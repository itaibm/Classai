# Year 5 Languages — Spanish (ages 10–11) — Scope & Sequence

> Part of **the best international primary school** (see [`../../../SCHOOL-CHARTER.md`](../../../SCHOOL-CHARTER.md)).
> Built to [`../../PLANNING-BRIEF.md`](../../PLANNING-BRIEF.md). Source material:
> `knowledge-base/subjects/languages/spanish-ks2.md`, `spanish-extension.md` and `resources.md`.
> Target level by the end of the year: **secure CEFR A1, moving into A2**.

## Year overview

Year 5 is the fifth year of Spanish, and the year pupils move from **connected sentences to short
paragraphs**. The big grammatical step is the **present tense as a system**: pupils learn to conjugate
regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs and the key irregulars *ser*, *tener*, *ir* and *hacer*, so that they can
talk about other people, not just themselves. They describe people with adjectives that agree in gender
and number, describe their home with *hay* and *está*, give opinions about school subjects with reasons,
narrate a daily routine with reflexive verbs, shop with numbers to 1,000, and — in the final units —
talk about future plans with *voy a* + infinitive and meet their first past-tense phrases (*fui*, *comí*,
*vi*) as fixed chunks.

Every unit keeps the four skills in balance: **listening and speaking first** (songs, games, role-plays),
then **reading** (short authentic and adapted texts), then **writing** (a paragraph at the end of each
unit, growing from 4 to 8 sentences). Pronunciation and phonics are practised briefly every lesson
(stress rules, written accents, *j/g*, *ll/y*, *ñ*, *r/rr*, *c/z* — noting that *c* before e/i and *z* are
said like "th" in much of Spain and like "s" in Latin America; both are correct). Intercultural learning
runs throughout, with a focus unit on the Spanish-speaking world (Peru, Argentina, Mexico, Spain,
Equatorial Guinea and more) and songs, festivals and stories from across it.

**Swappable language:** this structure works for any language. A school teaching Hebrew or French
instead should keep the same unit themes and skills progression and use
`knowledge-base/subjects/hebrew/kita-5-6.md` or `knowledge-base/subjects/french/` for the language content.

**What this year assumes (from Year 4):** greetings, numbers to 100, colours, family, animals, the
calendar and weather, food and opinions with *porque*, hobbies with *me gusta* + infinitive, the body
with *me duele*, telling the time, places in town and directions; sentences joined with *y*, *pero*,
*también*.

**What Year 6 relies on:** conjugating regular present-tense verbs and *ser/tener/ir/hacer*; adjective
agreement; opinions with reasons; the near future (*voy a…*); confident use of a bilingual dictionary; and
writing a paragraph of 6–8 accurate sentences — ready for the preterite past tense, longer texts and
simple conversations on familiar topics.

## Unit 1 — ¡Hola otra vez! — Me presento

**Essential question:** How much can I already say about myself — and how can I say it in a paragraph?

**Key vocabulary:** me llamo, tengo … años, vivo en, mi cumpleaños es el…, hablo, soy, me gusta(n), no me gusta(n), porque, y, pero, también, además

### Lesson 1 — ¿Qué recuerdas? What do you remember?
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can greet people and answer five personal questions in Spanish without notes." (KS2 languages / CEFR A1)
- **Hook:** A mystery Spanish-speaking pen-pal video message (teacher-made) asks, "¿Cómo te llamas? ¿Cuántos años tienes?"
- **Key activity:** Speed-conversations in pairs: name, age, where you live, birthday, likes. Teacher models with a puppet; pupils rotate partners every minute.
- **Check for understanding:** "Your partner answers 'Tengo once años.' What did you ask, and what did they tell you?" → ¿Cuántos años tienes? — they are eleven.
- **Differentiation:** support: question-and-answer cue cards with pictures / stretch: add a follow-up question to each answer (¿Y tú?, ¿Por qué?).
- **Materials:** puppet, question cards, timer, world map.
- **Joy:** speed-conversation carousel with music between rounds.

### Lesson 2 — Pronunciation power-up: stress and accents
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can use the stress rules to say new words correctly and explain why some words have a written accent." (KS2 languages — phonics)
- **Hook:** "Why does 'papá' mean dad but 'papa' mean potato?"
- **Key activity:** Teach the rules: words ending in a vowel, n or s are stressed on the second-to-last syllable (*CA-sa*, *HA-blan*); others on the last (*ha-BLAR*, *es-pa-ÑOL*); a written accent marks an exception (*mú-si-ca*, *ca-FÉ*). Clap and say lists.
- **Check for understanding:** "Where is the stress in 'ordenador', and why does it need no accent?" → On the last syllable (*-DOR*); it ends in r, so the rule already puts stress there.
- **Differentiation:** support: words already split into syllables / stretch: predict whether ten unseen words need a written accent.
- **Materials:** word cards, clapping rhythm cards, mini-whiteboards.
- **Joy:** "Stress stomp" — stamp on the stressed syllable.

### Lesson 3 — Opinions with reasons
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can give opinions with reasons about things I like, joining ideas with connectives." (KS2 languages / CEFR A1)
- **Hook:** "Me encanta el fútbol porque es emocionante, pero no me gusta el tenis porque es aburrido." — "How much can you understand?"
- **Key activity:** Revise opinion phrases (me encanta, me gusta, no me gusta, odio) and adjectives (divertido, aburrido, interesante, fácil, difícil, emocionante); build sentences on a 'sentence builder' grid; add *además* (moreover) and *sin embargo* (however).
- **Check for understanding:** "Why is it 'me gustan los perros' but 'me gusta el chocolate'?" → *Gustan* is used with a plural thing (the dogs are liked); *gusta* with a singular thing.
- **Differentiation:** support: sentence-builder grid with picture icons / stretch: build a sentence with three clauses and two different connectives.
- **Materials:** sentence-builder grids, picture cards, mini-whiteboards.
- **Joy:** "Opinion auction" — bid for the longest correct sentence.

### Lesson 4 — Reading: a pen-pal letter
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can read a short letter and find specific information and opinions." (KS2 languages — reading)
- **Hook:** A letter from "Lucía in Lima" arrives in an envelope with Peruvian stamps (replica).
- **Key activity:** Read the letter; use cognates and context to guess new words; answer true/false and find-the-Spanish questions.
- **Check for understanding:** "Find a word in the letter you didn't know but could guess. How did you guess it?" → A cognate (e.g. *interesante*) or context clue explained.
- **Differentiation:** support: letter with a glossary and pictures / stretch: find and translate every opinion with its reason.
- **Materials:** pen-pal letter (adapted text), glossary, highlighters, map of Peru.
- **Joy:** opening the "real" letter.

### Lesson 5 — Writing: Me presento
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can write a paragraph of five or six sentences about myself with opinions and connectives." (KS2 languages — writing)
- **Hook:** "Write back to Lucía."
- **Key activity:** Model a paragraph on the board; highlight structures; pupils draft with a writing frame, then check against a checklist (capital letters, accents, connectives, opinions with reasons).
- **Check for understanding:** "Point to one sentence where you used 'porque'. What reason did you give?" → A correct sentence with an opinion and reason.
- **Differentiation:** support: gapped writing frame / stretch: include a question to Lucía and a sentence about a family member.
- **Materials:** writing frames, sentence builders, dictionaries.
- **Joy:** letters are actually sent (to a partner school if possible).

### Lesson 6 — Unit 1 check: a mini-interview
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can take part in a short interview answering and asking questions." (KS2 languages — speaking)
- **Hook:** "Welcome to 'Radio Clase'. Today's guest: you!"
- **Key activity:** Pupils interview each other in pairs for a pretend radio show (recorded if possible); self-assess against can-do statements.
- **Check for understanding:** "Ask me one question in Spanish and answer mine with a reason." → A correctly formed question and an answer with *porque*.
- **Differentiation:** support: interview cue cards / stretch: interview without cards and ask a follow-up.
- **Materials:** microphone prop, recording device, can-do checklist.
- **Joy:** "Radio Clase" recordings.

**End-of-unit check:** Pupil writes a 5–6 sentence paragraph about themselves with two opinions and reasons, and answers five personal questions orally.

## Unit 2 — Mi familia y mis amigos — describing people

**Essential question:** How do I describe what people look like and what they are like — and why do Spanish adjectives change their endings?

**Key vocabulary:** ser (soy, eres, es, somos, sois, son), tener (tengo, tienes, tiene, tenemos, tenéis, tienen), alto/a, bajo/a, delgado/a, fuerte, pelo (largo, corto, rizado, liso, rubio, castaño, negro), ojos (azules, verdes, marrones), simpático/a, gracioso/a, inteligente, trabajador/a, tímido/a, hablador/a, mi mejor amigo/a

### Lesson 7 — Ser: who someone is
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can use all forms of 'ser' to say what people are like." (KS2 languages — grammar)
- **Hook:** A family photo of a famous (fictional) Spanish-speaking family. "¿Quién es?"
- **Key activity:** Teach the full conjugation of *ser* with actions (point to self, partner, others); personal pronouns (yo, tú, él, ella, nosotros, vosotros, ellos, ellas); note that *usted* and (in Latin America) *ustedes* are used for formal and plural 'you'.
- **Check for understanding:** "How would you say 'we are funny' and 'they are shy'?" → Somos graciosos; son tímidos.
- **Differentiation:** support: verb table with pictures of each person / stretch: explain when you'd use *usted* instead of *tú*.
- **Materials:** verb table poster, family photo, pronoun action cards.
- **Joy:** the *ser* rap with actions.

### Lesson 8 — Adjective agreement
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can make adjectives agree with the person or thing they describe." (KS2 languages — grammar)
- **Hook:** "Mi hermano es alto. Mi hermana es alt… what?"
- **Key activity:** Discover patterns with colour-coded cards: -o/-a/-os/-as (alto, alta, altos, altas); -e and most consonant endings don't change for gender (inteligente, fuerte, azul), but add -s/-es in plural; -or adds -a (trabajador → trabajadora).
- **Check for understanding:** "Correct this: 'Mis amigas son simpáticos.'" → *Simpáticas* — the friends are all female and plural, so the adjective ends in -as.
- **Differentiation:** support: -o/-a adjectives only with colour-coded cards / stretch: explain why a mixed group (boys and girls) takes the masculine plural.
- **Materials:** colour-coded adjective cards (blue/pink/purple for masculine/feminine/unchanging), mini-whiteboards.
- **Joy:** "Agreement snap" card game.

### Lesson 9 — Hair and eyes with tener
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can describe hair and eyes using 'tener' and adjectives placed after the noun." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** "Guess who? ¿Tiene el pelo rizado?"
- **Key activity:** Teach *tener* forms; *Tengo el pelo corto y castaño; tiene los ojos verdes*; notice adjectives come after the noun and 'ojos' is plural so colours are plural.
- **Check for understanding:** "Why is it 'ojos azules' and not 'ojos azul'?" → *Ojos* is plural, so the adjective must be plural too.
- **Differentiation:** support: picture-labelled word mats / stretch: describe a person using three physical and two personality adjectives in one sentence.
- **Materials:** 'Guess Who'-style boards (home-made), word mats.
- **Joy:** a home-made "¿Quién es?" guessing game.

### Lesson 10 — Personality and my best friend
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can describe someone's personality with qualifiers (muy, bastante, un poco)." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** "Is your best friend 'muy hablador' or 'un poco tímido'?"
- **Key activity:** Personality adjectives with mime; qualifiers; "Mi mejor amigo/amiga se llama… Es muy… pero un poco…". Pair work: describe your friend without saying the name; partner guesses.
- **Check for understanding:** "Translate: 'My best friend is quite hard-working but a bit shy.' (friend is female)." → Mi mejor amiga es bastante trabajadora pero un poco tímida.
- **Differentiation:** support: sentence-builder / stretch: add a reason ('porque siempre hace los deberes').
- **Materials:** mime cards, sentence builders.
- **Joy:** mime-and-guess personality game.

### Lesson 11 — Listening: who's who?
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can listen to descriptions and identify people from details." (KS2 languages — listening)
- **Hook:** "Solve the case: which suspect took the cake?"
- **Key activity:** Listen to recorded witness descriptions (native-speaker or teacher recordings) and match to suspect pictures; listen for negatives (*no es alto*) and qualifiers.
- **Check for understanding:** "The witness says 'no tiene el pelo largo'. Which suspects can you rule out?" → All those with long hair.
- **Differentiation:** support: fewer suspects and a word mat / stretch: note two details per suspect that were NOT mentioned.
- **Materials:** audio recordings, suspect cards, answer grids.
- **Joy:** the "who took the cake?" mystery.

### Lesson 12 — -ar, -er, -ir verbs: vivir and comer
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can conjugate regular -er and -ir verbs in the present tense for all persons." (KS2 languages — grammar)
- **Hook:** "If 'vivo' means 'I live', how do you say 'we live'?"
- **Key activity:** Pattern-spotting with *comer* (como, comes, come, comemos, coméis, comen) and *vivir* (vivo, vives, vive, vivimos, vivís, viven); compare the -er and -ir endings; practise with family sentences ("Mis abuelos viven en…").
- **Check for understanding:** "What is the only difference between the -er and -ir endings in the present tense?" → The 'we' and 'you plural' forms: -emos/-éis (comer) vs -imos/-ís (vivir).
- **Differentiation:** support: verb wheels to turn / stretch: conjugate a new verb (*escribir*, *beber*) without help.
- **Materials:** verb wheels (card and split pins), verb tables.
- **Joy:** "Verb wheel races".

### Lesson 13 — Reading: a family description from Argentina
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can read a longer description and answer questions using detail." (KS2 languages — reading)
- **Hook:** "Mateo from Buenos Aires describes his family — including his grandmother who dances tango."
- **Key activity:** Read an adapted text; use a bilingual dictionary to look up three new words (teach the dictionary skill: finding the infinitive of a verb form); answer comprehension questions in English and Spanish.
- **Check for understanding:** "You see 'baila' in the text. What word do you look up in the dictionary, and why?" → *Bailar* — dictionaries list verbs in the infinitive.
- **Differentiation:** support: text with glossary / stretch: find three verbs and name the infinitive and the person for each.
- **Materials:** adapted text, bilingual dictionaries, map of Argentina.
- **Joy:** a short tango music clip and a two-step try.

### Lesson 14 — Speaking: presenting a family member
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can present a family member or friend in four or five sentences." (KS2 languages — speaking)
- **Hook:** "Bring a photo (or draw a portrait) of someone important to you."
- **Key activity:** Prepare a short oral presentation (name, age, where they live, looks, personality, what they like); rehearse with partner feedback on pronunciation.
- **Check for understanding:** "Which adjective in your presentation needed to agree, and how did you make it agree?" → A named adjective and the correct ending explained.
- **Differentiation:** support: cue cards with a model / stretch: include a comparison (*más alto que…*).
- **Materials:** photos or drawings, cue cards.
- **Joy:** "Galería de familia" photo gallery walk.

### Lesson 15 — Writing: my best friend
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can write a description of a friend with correct adjective agreement and verbs in the third person." (KS2 languages — writing)
- **Hook:** "Write a 'wanted' poster for the world's best friend."
- **Key activity:** Draft a 6-sentence description (third person: *es, tiene, vive, le gusta*); peer-check agreement with a colour-coding checklist.
- **Check for understanding:** "Why do you write 'le gusta' and not 'me gusta' when describing your friend?" → *Le gusta* means 'he/she likes'; *me gusta* is 'I like'.
- **Differentiation:** support: gapped frame / stretch: include one sentence in the 'we' form about what you do together.
- **Materials:** poster paper, checklists, dictionaries.
- **Joy:** 'se busca' (wanted) posters displayed.

### Lesson 16 — Unit 2 check
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can show I can describe people in speech and writing." (KS2 languages — assessment)
- **Hook:** "Describe a famous person — the class guesses who."
- **Key activity:** Listening task (identify people), reading task (true/false), writing task (describe a person, 5 sentences), and a speaking guessing game.
- **Check for understanding:** "Describe a famous person in three sentences using 'es' and 'tiene'." → Three accurate sentences with agreement.
- **Differentiation:** support: word mat / stretch: add personality with qualifiers and a reason.
- **Materials:** assessment tasks, famous-person picture cards.
- **Joy:** "¿Quién soy?" celebrity guessing game.

**End-of-unit check:** Pupil conjugates *ser* and *tener*, writes a description of a person with correct adjective agreement, and understands a spoken description.

## Unit 3 — Mi casa — my home

**Essential question:** How do I describe where I live and where things are?

**Key vocabulary:** vivo en una casa / un piso, en el campo, en la ciudad, la cocina, el salón, el comedor, el dormitorio, el cuarto de baño, el jardín, hay, no hay, está, encima de, debajo de, al lado de, delante de, detrás de, entre, la cama, el armario, la mesa, la silla, la estantería

### Lesson 17 — ¿Dónde vives?
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can say what kind of home I live in and where it is." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** Photos of homes across the Spanish-speaking world: a flat in Madrid, a house in the Andes, a colourful house in Cartagena (Colombia).
- **Key activity:** Vocabulary for homes and locations; *Vivo en un piso en la ciudad*; *Vivo en una casa en el campo/en la costa/en las montañas*. Survey the class.
- **Check for understanding:** "Why is it 'una casa' but 'un piso'?" → *Casa* is feminine; *piso* is masculine — every noun has a gender.
- **Differentiation:** support: picture-labelled options / stretch: add an opinion about where you live with a reason.
- **Materials:** home photographs, survey sheets.
- **Joy:** "Dream home" vote.

### Lesson 18 — Rooms with hay
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can list rooms in my home using 'hay' and numbers." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** "En mi casa hay… ¡un castillo!" (a silly model).
- **Key activity:** Room vocabulary; *hay* (there is/are) and *no hay*; plural articles (*dos dormitorios*). Label a floor plan.
- **Check for understanding:** "Translate: 'In my flat there are three bedrooms but there isn't a garden.'" → En mi piso hay tres dormitorios pero no hay jardín.
- **Differentiation:** support: a labelled floor plan / stretch: add *también* and *además* to describe a large house.
- **Materials:** floor plans, room flashcards.
- **Joy:** design your dream house floor plan in Spanish.

### Lesson 19 — Prepositions: where is it?
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can describe where objects are using 'está' and prepositions." (KS2 languages — grammar)
- **Hook:** "¿Dónde está el gato?" — a toy cat hides around the room.
- **Key activity:** Prepositions with actions (encima de, debajo de, al lado de, delante de, detrás de, entre); *está* for location; note that *de + el* becomes *del* (*al lado del armario*).
- **Check for understanding:** "Why is it 'al lado del armario' and not 'al lado de el armario'?" → *De* + *el* combine to make *del*.
- **Differentiation:** support: picture cards showing each preposition / stretch: describe the positions of three objects relative to each other in one sentence.
- **Materials:** toy cat, box, picture cards.
- **Joy:** "Hide the cat" game.

### Lesson 20 — My bedroom
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can describe my bedroom, including furniture, colours and positions." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** "Is your bedroom tidy? ¿Está ordenado o desordenado?"
- **Key activity:** Furniture vocabulary; describe a picture of a bedroom; listening activity — draw a bedroom from a description (dictado de dibujo).
- **Check for understanding:** "Listen: 'La lámpara está encima de la mesa, al lado de la cama.' Where do you draw the lamp?" → On the table, next to the bed.
- **Differentiation:** support: fewer objects in the drawing dictation / stretch: describe your own bedroom in six sentences including colour agreement.
- **Materials:** drawing sheets, audio or teacher reading, furniture flashcards.
- **Joy:** drawing dictation.

### Lesson 21 — Homes around the Spanish-speaking world
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can compare homes in different Spanish-speaking places and give opinions." (KS2 languages — intercultural)
- **Hook:** "A patio in Seville, a house on stilts by a Colombian river, a Buenos Aires apartment. Which would you choose?"
- **Key activity:** Read short captions about homes (climate, style, family life); compare with pupils' homes; opinions with reasons.
- **Check for understanding:** "Give an opinion about one home with a reason in Spanish." → e.g. Me gusta la casa de Sevilla porque tiene un patio bonito.
- **Differentiation:** support: captions with pictures and glossary / stretch: explain in Spanish one reason why a home is built that way (e.g. for the heat).
- **Materials:** photo captions, map of the Spanish-speaking world.
- **Joy:** "Home-swap" daydream vote.

### Lesson 22 — Reading: an estate agent advert
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can read property adverts and find key information." (KS2 languages — reading)
- **Hook:** "Se vende: castillo con piscina y dragón."
- **Key activity:** Read four short adverts (adapted, with numbers and prices); match families to the right home; spot the silly one.
- **Check for understanding:** "Which home suits a family with three children and a dog? Give two reasons from the advert." → A named home with two details (e.g. three bedrooms, a garden).
- **Differentiation:** support: adverts with picture icons / stretch: write your own advert with a surprising feature.
- **Materials:** adapted adverts, family profile cards.
- **Joy:** writing silly adverts.

### Lesson 23 — Writing: my home
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can write a paragraph describing my home with 'hay', 'está' and opinions." (KS2 languages — writing)
- **Hook:** "Describe your home for Lucía in Lima."
- **Key activity:** Draft 6–7 sentences; include a room list with *hay*, a position with *está*, colours with agreement and an opinion with a reason; peer-check.
- **Check for understanding:** "Show one sentence with 'hay' and one with 'está'. Why is each verb used?" → *Hay* introduces what exists; *está* says where something is.
- **Differentiation:** support: writing frame / stretch: add a sentence about what you do in one room (*En el salón veo la tele*).
- **Materials:** writing frames, dictionaries.
- **Joy:** illustrated "Mi casa" booklets.

### Lesson 24 — Unit 3 check
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can show I can describe a home and the position of things." (KS2 languages — assessment)
- **Hook:** "Estate agent challenge: sell your house in 60 seconds."
- **Key activity:** Listening, reading and writing mini-tasks; speaking: a 60-second 'sales pitch' for a home.
- **Check for understanding:** "Describe where three objects are in this picture." → Three accurate sentences with *está* and prepositions.
- **Differentiation:** support: picture prompts / stretch: include a comparison between two rooms.
- **Materials:** assessment tasks, room pictures.
- **Joy:** the estate agent pitches.

**End-of-unit check:** Pupil writes a paragraph describing their home using *hay*, *está* and prepositions, and follows a spoken description to draw a room.

## Unit 4 — El colegio — school, opinions and -ar verbs

**Essential question:** How can I talk about my school day and give reasons for my opinions?

**Key vocabulary:** las asignaturas, las matemáticas, el inglés, las ciencias, la historia, la geografía, el arte, la música, la educación física, la informática, el español, el horario, el recreo, la comida, el profesor / la profesora, hablar, estudiar, escuchar, llevar, practicar, útil, práctico/a, fácil, difícil, divertido/a, aburrido/a

### Lesson 25 — School subjects
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can name school subjects with the correct article and say which I study." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** "Which subject has the same name in Spanish and English (almost)?"
- **Key activity:** Subject vocabulary with cognates; articles (*las matemáticas*, *la música*, *el arte*); *Estudio…*.
- **Check for understanding:** "Why is it 'me gustan las matemáticas' but 'me gusta la música'?" → *Matemáticas* is plural, so *gustan*; *música* is singular, so *gusta*.
- **Differentiation:** support: picture subject cards / stretch: identify cognates and 'false friends' in school vocabulary.
- **Materials:** subject flashcards, school timetable.
- **Joy:** subject bingo.

### Lesson 26 — Opinions about subjects with reasons
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can give opinions about subjects with reasons, using adjectives that agree." (KS2 languages)
- **Hook:** "¿Cuál es tu asignatura favorita? ¿Por qué?"
- **Key activity:** *Me encanta la historia porque es interesante; odio las ciencias porque son difíciles* — agreement in reasons; class survey and bar chart (maths link).
- **Check for understanding:** "Why is it 'son difíciles' when talking about 'las ciencias'?" → *Ciencias* is plural, so the verb is *son* and the adjective is plural.
- **Differentiation:** support: opinion sentence builder / stretch: compare two subjects (*más interesante que*).
- **Materials:** sentence builders, survey sheets.
- **Joy:** class favourite-subject chart.

### Lesson 27 — -ar verbs: the pattern
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can conjugate regular -ar verbs in the present tense." (KS2 languages — grammar)
- **Hook:** "Hablo, hablas, habla… can you continue the pattern?"
- **Key activity:** Conjugate *hablar* (hablo, hablas, habla, hablamos, habláis, hablan); apply to *estudiar*, *escuchar*, *practicar*, *llevar*; verb-ending dice game.
- **Check for understanding:** "How do you say 'we study science' and 'they listen to music'?" → Estudiamos ciencias; escuchan música.
- **Differentiation:** support: verb wheel for -ar verbs / stretch: mix -ar, -er and -ir verbs in one game.
- **Materials:** verb tables, dice with persons and verbs.
- **Joy:** verb-ending dice battle.

- **Key activity:** Revise weather (hace sol, hace calor, hace frío, llueve, nieva); build conditional sentences with *si* + present + future (*si hace sol, voy a ir a la playa*).
- **Check for understanding:** "Translate: 'If it snows, we are going to ski.'" → Si nieva, vamos a esquiar.
- **Differentiation:** support: weather picture cards with sentence frames / stretch: create a weather-forecast holiday plan for three days.
- **Materials:** weather cards, forecast maps.
- **Joy:** "Weather-forecast presenter" performances.

### Lesson 62 — Reading: holiday postcards
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can read postcards and identify present and future time frames." (KS2 languages — reading)
- **Hook:** Real-looking postcards from Cuba, the Canary Islands and Patagonia.
- **Key activity:** Read three postcards; highlight present tense in one colour and future (*voy a…*) in another; answer questions.
- **Check for understanding:** "Find one thing the writer is doing now and one thing they are going to do." → Two correct examples with the verb forms identified.
- **Differentiation:** support: postcards with glossary / stretch: find a past-tense phrase (e.g. *ayer fui…*) and guess its meaning from context.
- **Materials:** postcards (adapted), highlighters.
- **Joy:** decode the 'secret' postcard.

### Lesson 63 — First steps into the past: fui, comí, vi
- **Duration:** 35 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can use three past-tense phrases to say what I did last weekend." (KS2 languages — preview of Year 6)
- **Hook:** "Ayer fui al cine, comí palomitas y vi una película. ¿Qué hiciste tú?"
- **Key activity:** Learn *fui* (I went), *comí* (I ate), *vi* (I saw) and *fue* (it was) as chunks; time phrases (*ayer*, *el fin de semana pasado*); compare with present and future forms on a timeline.
- **Check for understanding:** "Put these on a timeline: 'voy', 'fui', 'voy a ir'." → *fui* (past), *voy* (present), *voy a ir* (future).
- **Differentiation:** support: picture timeline with the three chunks / stretch: add *me gustó* (I liked it) with a reason.
- **Materials:** timeline strips, picture cards.
- **Joy:** "Weekend news" circle.

### Lesson 64 — Listening: travel announcements and plans
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can understand spoken travel information and plans." (KS2 languages — listening)
- **Hook:** "Atención, pasajeros: el tren a Sevilla sale a las diez y cuarto…"
- **Key activity:** Listen to station and airport announcements (times, platforms, destinations) and a conversation about holiday plans; complete grids.
- **Check for understanding:** "At what time does the train to Seville leave, and from which platform?" → The time and platform from the recording (e.g. 10:15, platform 3).
- **Differentiation:** support: multiple-choice grid / stretch: note an extra detail from each announcement.
- **Materials:** recordings, grids.
- **Joy:** "Station announcer" — pupils make their own announcements.

### Lesson 65 — Writing: my holiday plans (and one memory)
- **Duration:** 40 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can write a paragraph using present, future and at least one past-tense phrase." (KS2 languages — writing)
- **Hook:** "Write a postcard to Lucía."
- **Key activity:** Write a postcard (8 sentences): where you usually go on holiday (present), next summer's plans (future), one past memory (*el verano pasado fui a…*), weather and opinions.
- **Check for understanding:** "Label each verb in your postcard as past, present or future." → Correct labels.
- **Differentiation:** support: postcard frame with a time-frame colour key / stretch: include two past-tense chunks and a *si* sentence.
- **Materials:** postcard templates, verb reference cards.
- **Joy:** real postcards posted to a partner school.

### Lesson 66 — Unit 8 check
- **Duration:** 30 min
- **Objective:** "By the end, I can show I can talk about holidays in three time frames." (KS2 languages — assessment)
- **Hook:** "Travel agent role-play."
- **Key activity:** Listening, reading and writing tasks; speaking role-play: a travel agent asks about past and future holidays.
- **Check for understanding:** "Tell me where you are going to go next summer and one thing you did last summer." → A *voy a* sentence and a *fui* sentence.
- **Differentiation:** support: role-play cards / stretch: add weather, transport and an opinion.
- **Materials:** assessment tasks, role-play props.
- **Joy:** travel agency role-play.

**End-of-unit check:** Pupil conjugates *ir*, uses *voy a* + infinitive for plans, uses two past-tense chunks, and writes a postcard in three time frames.

- **Differentiation:** support: shorter presentation with cue cards / stretch: present without notes.

### Lesson 70 — Year review and celebration

- ***Donde viven los monstruos*** (Spanish edition of *Where the Wild Things Are*) by Maurice Sendak — a familiar story that lets pupils use prior knowledge to read Spanish.

## End-of-year mastery checks

1. **Present tense** — conjugates regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs and *ser*, *tener*, *ir* and *hacer* for all persons (seen in a verb game and in writing).
2. **Agreement** — makes adjectives, colours and nationalities agree in gender and number.
3. **Opinions** — gives opinions with reasons and connectives across all topics.
4. **Three time frames** — uses the present, *voy a* + infinitive, and at least two past-tense chunks correctly.
5. **Listening** — understands a short spoken passage on a familiar topic, including times, prices and opinions.
6. **Reading** — reads a short adapted text and finds detail, using a bilingual dictionary where needed.
7. **Writing** — writes an accurate paragraph of 6–8 sentences with varied structures.
8. **Speaking** — gives a 1–2 minute presentation and answers a question spontaneously.
9. **Intercultural** — locates Spanish-speaking countries and describes one festival or tradition, recognising regional variation in Spanish.

## Teacher guidance

**Common misconceptions to watch for**
- *"Me gusta" + plural noun* ("me gusta los perros"). Teach *gustan* for plural things.
- *Adding "es" for "is" everywhere* ("es en el jardín"). Location uses *está*; *hay* for 'there is/are'.
- *Mixing subject pronouns and endings* ("yo habla"). The verb ending shows the person; pronouns are often dropped.
- *Adjective before noun by default* ("azul ojos"). Most adjectives follow the noun.
- *Capitalising days, months, nationalities and languages.* In Spanish these are lower case.
- *"Seseo" and "ceceo" confusion.* Both pronunciations of *c/z* are correct; choose one model and be consistent.
- *Word-for-word translation* ("tengo 11 años" is right; "soy 11" is wrong). Teach chunks and explain why.

**How to teach it well**
- Keep lessons mainly in Spanish, with English for grammar explanations and reflection.
- Listening and speaking first; reading and writing follow. Use songs, games and role-play daily.
- Recycle vocabulary across units (colours in clothes, numbers in prices, weather in holidays).
- Teach verb patterns explicitly with visual verb tables; pupils should see the system, not just memorise chunks.
- Use authentic materials where possible (songs, menus, signs, short videos) and real correspondence with children in Spanish-speaking countries.

**Vertical connections**
- *From Year 4:* chunks for opinions, hobbies, food, time and directions; the habit of joining sentences with connectives.
- *Into Year 6:* the full preterite past tense for common verbs, longer descriptive and narrative paragraphs, conversations on familiar topics, and reading short authentic stories.
- *Across subjects:* geography (Spanish-speaking countries, markets), music ("De colores", Andean music), maths (numbers to 1,000, prices, timetables), history (Maya link), English (grammar terminology: verbs, adjectives, agreement).
