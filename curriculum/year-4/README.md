## The year in one paragraph

Year 4 is the year children become **reasoners, critics and makers**. In maths they master numbers to a million, long multiplication, fractions, decimals to three places, a first look at percentages, and angles measured with a protractor. In English they read **whole novels** from four continents, discuss them like critics, and write stories, reports, letters and persuasive pieces with deliberate grammatical choices. In science they own the **whole scientific method** — planning fair tests with controlled variables — across materials, Earth and space, forces and life cycles. History looks at **four civilisations around AD 900** (Vikings, Baghdad, Benin, the Maya), geography at a **restless, working planet** (tectonics, rivers, a South American region study), and Spanish moves from single sentences to **connected opinions with reasons**. Art, music, PE, computing and life skills add perspective drawing, printmaking, clay and textiles; ukulele chords and world music; swimming, sport, dance and orienteering; programs that decide and remember; and money sense, sustainability and service.

## Sample weekly timetable

Lessons are 30–40 minutes (maths and English up to 40; others 30–40). Mornings hold the core subjects while children are freshest; afternoons are for practical and active work.

| | Session 1 | Session 2 | Break | Session 3 | Lunch | Session 4 | Session 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | English | Maths | outdoor play | Science | outdoor play | Languages | Music |
| **Tuesday** | English | Maths | outdoor play | History | outdoor play | PE & Health | — |
| **Wednesday** | English | Maths | outdoor play | Science | outdoor play | Computing | Art & Design |
| **Thursday** | English | Maths | outdoor play | Languages | outdoor play | Geography | — |
| **Friday** | English | Maths | outdoor play | Life Skills | outdoor play | PE & Health | — |

**Every day, outside the lesson count:** 20+ minutes of independent reading plus the class novel read aloud; 10 minutes of spelling practice; a 5-minute maths fluency starter (built into the maths lesson); a Spanish greeting and date routine; and unstructured outdoor play. The shorter afternoons on Tuesday, Thursday and Friday leave room for clubs, swimming travel, reading and play — in line with the charter's commitment to wellbeing and childhood.

A homeschool or tutor-led week can follow the same pattern: two core sessions every morning, then one or two of the other subjects in the afternoon.

## Threads that connect the subjects

- **Around AD 900** — history's Vikings, Baghdad, Benin and Maya link to Benin-inspired clay plaques and Islamic geometric pattern (art), Maya base-20 numbers and al-Khwarizmi (maths), trade routes and rivers (geography).
- **Earth and space** — science's Solar System and day/night link to time zones (geography and maths), Holst's *The Planets* (music), *Cosmic* (English) and rocket explanations.
- **Energy and forces** — science's forces and mechanisms link to *The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind* (English), windmills and sustainability (life skills), and earthquake-resistant design (geography).
- **Mexico, Peru and the Spanish-speaking world** — *Esperanza Rising* (English), Día de los Muertos and "La Bamba" (Spanish and music), the Andes and Amazon region study (geography), the Maya (history), Andean textiles (art).
- **Data** — maths line graphs and percentages, science investigations, spreadsheets (computing), fitness retests (PE), and the waste audit (life skills).
- **Fairness and courage** — *Journey to Jo'burg* and "Siyahamba", children's rights, the Benin Bronzes debate, persuasive letters and the service project.

## How Year 4 fits the spine

- **Builds on Year 3** ([`../year-3/`](../year-3/)): place value to 10,000, all tables to 12 × 12, tenths and hundredths; fronted adverbials and possessive apostrophes; states of matter and circuits; ancient Greece and Rome; climate zones and fieldwork basics; recorder and stave reading; loops in Scratch.
- **Prepares for Year 5** ([`../year-5/`](../year-5/)): numbers to 10,000,000, long division, all four operations with fractions, ratio and algebra; the passive voice, semicolons and formal register; inheritance and evolution, light, circuits; Tudors, the Atlantic trade and the Second World War; contour maps and economic geography; the past and future tenses in Spanish; keyboards and ensembles; Python.

## Assessment across the year

Every unit ends with a short, low-stakes **end-of-unit check**; each subject file lists **end-of-year mastery checks** with how to see them; maths and English hold a retrieval-and-assessment week at the end of each term. Art is assessed by looking at and discussing work; PE by observing performance and personal bests; languages by speaking, listening, reading and writing tasks. Results feed the next unit's teaching and each child's learner profile.
