# Lesson 1 — My sketchbook is my workshop
**Art & Design · Year 1 · Unit 1 (Sketchbook habits & line revisited)** · 25 min · **Delivery: 🤖 AI-led, with a hands-on making beat**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can set up and date a sketchbook page and explain it is for testing, not for perfect pictures."
*(Human view of [`lesson-01-my-sketchbook-is-my-workshop.json`](./lesson-01-my-sketchbook-is-my-workshop.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

**Have ready:** a sketchbook (or sheets stapled together), pencil, fine-liner or felt-tip, crayons or coloured pencils. For support, pre-draw a small date box and a 6-square grid.

## Flow

- **Hook (0–2):** "Today your sketchbook becomes your artist workshop" — crossings-out, wild lines and surprising marks are welcome.
- **Explain (2–6):** a sketchbook is a workshop in book form — for testing marks, practising lines, collecting ideas and remembering what you tried. The **date** shows when you made the page, so you can see your progress.
- **Example (6–10):** the AI models a date box and a warm-up strip of different marks. Rule for today: **no rubbing out** — a surprising mark is evidence of experimenting.
- **Check (10–13):** "What is a sketchbook really for?" — trap handled: "only for neat, finished pictures."
- **Practice — hands-on (13–23):** the child dates a page and fills it with as many different marks as possible (dots, dashes, zig-zags, waves, loops, soft and hard lines) in pencil, fine-liner and crayon. The adult hands back to the AI with "We're ready to recap."
- **Recap (23–25):** the win is not neatness — it's bravery, trying and a page full of ideas.

**Adaptivity:** 12-item bank (purpose, date, "no rubbing out" mindset, test-page features, set-up order, sketchbook vs finished work, reflective note) · start level 2 · worry about neatness → level 1.

**Differentiation:** support — pre-drawn date box and grid, six named mark prompts, adult scribes the date · stretch — write a tiny artist note beside one mark ("I pressed hard…").
**Evidence (assessed by looking and talking):** a dated page with many different marks, and the child can say a sketchbook is for testing and experimenting, not only perfect pictures.
