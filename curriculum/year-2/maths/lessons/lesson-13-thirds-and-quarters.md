# Lesson 13 — Thirds and quarters of shapes
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 3 (Fractions)** · 24 min · **Delivery: 🧑‍🏫 human intro → 🤖 AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can find ⅓, ¼ and ¾ of a shape and explain that the parts must be equal."
*(Human view of [`lesson-13-thirds-and-quarters.json`](./lesson-13-thirds-and-quarters.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

## Your part (first ~8 minutes — real paper in real hands)

**Lay out before the lesson:** paper strips + circles, colouring pencils, scissors, a real chocolate bar (or a paper one in squares). Keep strips in reach afterwards — the AI asks for folding again.

- **Hook (0–3):** three friends, one chocolate bar. Mime an unfair break (one giant piece, two crumbs) and let the child protest. Retrieval: what made a piece a REAL half last year? (equal parts)
- **Explain (3–8):** fold a strip into **thirds** like a letter into an envelope (⅓ — "the bottom number says how many equal parts"); fold another into **quarters** (half, then half again); shade 3 of 4 → **¾**. Then the magic: lay the third-strip beside the quarter-strip — *more pieces means each piece is SMALLER*.
- **Handoff line (say as written):** "Right, now my friend on the screen is going to draw some fraction bars for us. Watch!"

## The AI's part (8–24)

- **Example (8–13):** animated fraction bars mirror the real strips; child directs the shading of ⅓ and names ¾. Trap handled: "3 shaded so it's ⅓" → the bottom number counts ALL parts.
- **Check (13–17):** the heart — "which is bigger, ½ or ¼?" Child must PROVE it with the real strips (fold, overlay). Wrong-answer playbook: "¼ because 4 > 2" → fold the same strip both ways.
- **Practice (17–21):** adaptive 10-item bank — name halves/thirds/quarters, equal-parts trueFalse (level 1); pizza thirds, impostor thirds ("one big, two tiny"), ¾ fillBlank, ½ vs ¼, flag "what's NOT red?" (level 2); stretch: ⅓ vs ¼, inspector sorting (real vs impostor splits), "convince a friend ⅓ isn't bigger than ½."
- **Recap (21–24):** child states both rules — equal parts; more parts = smaller parts — then the chocolate bar gets shared fairly in three for real.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach + fold-and-overlay a real strip + level 1 · mastery = 4+ correct incl. one impostor spotted and one size comparison explained.

**Video (reinforce):** [Fractions Are a Piece of Cake - Full Episode | Series 8 E24 | Numberblocks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsnJtCloGf0) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: shout EQUAL or NOT EQUAL at every split in the party.

**Differentiation:** support — fold the same bar into 2 vs 3 to feel it; pre-creased strips · stretch — unequal-split challenge, ¾ two ways.
**Evidence:** folds/shades correct thirds and quarters, identifies ¾, rejects an unequal split, explains ½ > ¼.
