# Lesson 15 — Pounds, pence and making amounts
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 4 (Money: pounds and pence)** · 22 min · **Delivery: 🧑‍🏫 human intro → 🤖 AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can show that £1 = 100p and make an amount the efficient way."
*(Human view of [`lesson-15-money-making-amounts.json`](./lesson-15-money-making-amounts.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

## Your part (first ~8 minutes, REAL coins)

**Lay out before the lesson:** a tray of plastic coins (lots of 1p, 2p, 5p, 10p, 20p, 50p, £1, £2), a clear "pound pot" jar with a heap of 1p coins beside it, price labels, a mini-whiteboard. Keep the coin tray in reach — the AI asks the child to build with real coins later.

- **Hook (0–3):** "How many pennies make a whole pound?" — count pennies into the pot in tens together. Puzzle: tiny 5p vs big 2p — which is worth more?
- **Explain (3–8):** £1 = 100p; swap two 50p coins for £1; build 80p the clever way (50p + 20p + 10p — three coins, not eighty); write £1.50 — the dot splits pounds from pence.
- **Handoff line:** *"Right, now my friend on the screen is going to draw the coins we just used. Watch!"* → the AI takes over.

## AI part

- **Example (8–13):** character redraws the 80p build (animated coin circles); child makes 65p with the fewest coins using the real tray — 50, 60, 65.
- **Check (13–17):** "How many pence in £1?" + the diagnostic: **£1.50 = 1 pound and 50 pence** (not five!). Wrong-answer playbook covers the £1.05/£1.50 swap and £-vs-p mixing.
- **Practice (17–20):** adaptive 10-item bank — value-not-size and "three 10p coins = 30p not 3p" (level 1), fewest-coins builds, count-ups and £1.05 reading (level 2), stretch: fewest coins for 99p (6), £1.35, and "is the big 2p worth more?" explain-why.
- **Recap (20–22):** child states both rules: £1 = 100p; biggest coin first. Process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → reteach + real coins laid big-to-small + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a fewest-coins build and a £.pp reading.

**Optional video (reinforce):** [Working With Money | KS1 Maths Year2 | Home Learning — Explore Planet English](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TzlR77WkZXM) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: say each coin's value before the video does.

**Differentiation:** support — £1.50 vs £1.05 built side by side, coins pre-sorted big to small · stretch — £1.35 fewest coins, 99p in 6 coins.
**Evidence:** states £1 = 100p, builds amounts fewest-first, reads £.pp incl. £1.05.
