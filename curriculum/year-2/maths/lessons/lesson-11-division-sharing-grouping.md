# Lesson 11 — Division as sharing and grouping
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 2 (Calculation)** · 24 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can divide by sharing equally and by making equal groups."
*(Human view of [`lesson-11-division-sharing-grouping.json`](./lesson-11-division-sharing-grouping.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed — real counters are a nice optional extra.)*

## Flow (all AI, "fair dealer" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** 20 sweets, 5 friends — "share them so it's totally FAIR, or there'll be tears!" Child proposes a method. Retrieval: 4 × 5 = 20.
- **Explain (3–9):** animated dealing — one to each plate, round and round, until gone; every plate holds 4. "20 ÷ 5 = 4 — the OPPOSITE of 4 × 5 = 20."
- **Example (9–14):** division's other face — grouping: pack 20 into bags of 5, count the BAGS (4). Same fact, two stories. Traps handled: counting inside a bag instead of the bags; ÷ read as −.
- **Check (14–17):** "share 20 between 5 — how many each?" The key diagnostic: '5 each' = guessed from the number of friends, never dealt. Probe the 4 × 5 link.
- **Practice (17–21):** adaptive 10-item bank alternating sharing and grouping — 6÷3, 8÷2, pairs of shoes (level 1), 12÷3, party bags of 2, 20÷10, inverse fillBlank (level 2), and stretch: the 3/10/30 fact family, "Sam shares, Mia groups — who's right?" (both!), and 13 between 4 with one left over.
- **Recap (21–24):** child names the two faces of division + the inverse link; process praise ("you dealt fairly instead of guessing").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (level 3 = interleaved) · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with one-at-a-time dealing + level 1 · mastery = 4+ correct incl. a share, a group, and a ×-linked fact.

**Video:** none — the dealing animation is the teaching visual (per the KB honesty rule, no invented links).

**Differentiation:** support — deal one-to-each like cards, labelled plates · stretch — both ÷ facts from one ×, remainder talk.
**Evidence:** shares and groups a quantity correctly and links the quotient to a multiplication fact.
