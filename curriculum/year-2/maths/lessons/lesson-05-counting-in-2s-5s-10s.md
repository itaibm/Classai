## Flow (all AI, "super-speed counting" game frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** counting socks one-by-one is slow… but socks come in pairs! 2, 4, 6, 8 — super-speed. Retrieval: after 30 in tens?
- **Explain (3–7):** animated number line with equal jumps of 5; the one rule — every jump the SAME size — then the secret badges: 5s end in 0/5, 10s end in 0, 2s are even.
- **Example (7–11):** child counts five virtual 5p coins in 5s → 25p, fills the last jump (`5, 10, 15, 20, ___`).
- **Check (11–14):** the key diagnostic — "counting in 5s, could you EVER say 23?" → no; 23 has no 0-or-5 badge. Wrong-answer playbook: "yes" (no pattern) and uneven jumps (5, 10, 14, 20).
- **Practice (14–17):** adaptive 10-item bank — fill-gap sequences in 2s/5s/10s, the 17-impostor odd-one-out, a 5s-club categorize (23/42/51 never said), stretch into 3s and backwards 10s.
- **Recap (17–18):** child names one pattern; process praise ("you used the pattern to catch your own slips").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with concrete equal groups + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a pattern-check item and two different steps.

**Video (reinforce, ✅ verified):** [The Counting by Twos Song | Counting Songs | Scratch Garden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GvTcpfSnOMQ) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: clap on every number — they're all even; catch the last-digit pattern.

**Differentiation:** support — make the groups physically, say the step each jump, stay on 10s · stretch — 3s to 30, backwards 10s from 50.
**Evidence:** completes 2s/5s/10s sequences and uses a last-digit pattern to reject 23.
