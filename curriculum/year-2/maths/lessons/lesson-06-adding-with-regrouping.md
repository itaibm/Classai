## Flow (all AI, "the great exchange" shop frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** toy shop — a 36p car AND a 27p sticker pack. How much altogether? Retrieval: partition 36.
- **Explain (3–10):** animated whiteboard builds 36 + 27 in rods and cubes. Ones first: 6 + 7 = 13 → **the great exchange** (ten ones melt into one new ten, 3 stay) → tens: 30 + 20 + 10 = 60 → **63**. Anchor: "Ten ones become one ten — trade them in."
- **Example (10–16):** shared 27 + 46 — the child calls the exchange and states "7 tens and 3 ones — 73". Traps handled: "613" (no exchange) and "63" (dropped the carried ten).
- **Check (16–20):** solo 45 + 38 → 83. Wrong-answer playbook: **"713"** (the KB no-exchange error) and "73" (lost the carried ten).
- **Practice (20–25):** adaptive 10-item bank — ones-first and when-to-trade (level 1), sums with exchange + a no-exchange decision (42 + 35) + a ones-digit-fingerprint matchPairs (level 2), stretch: three numbers (12+23+14), the partition method (50 + 13), and "explain Sam's 713".
- **Recap (25–28):** child states the rule — trade when the ones make ten or more; process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → slow-motion exchange re-teach + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. one with and one without exchange.
