## Flow (all AI, animated two-sided clock on the whiteboard)

- **Hook (0–3):** "After half past we stop counting UP and start counting DOWN — why?" Retrieval: 'past' side + counting in 5s (Lesson 19).
- **Explain (3–9):** the LEFT side (6 round to 12) is the 'TO' side — count the minutes LEFT to the next hour. Hand on the 8: 5, 10, 15, 20 → **20 to, not 40 past**. Hand on the 9 → quarter to. Anchor: "Past the 6 → count what's LEFT → say '___ to'."
- **Example (9–14):** child counts 9→12 (15 left, quarter to), then reads the hand on the 10 (10 to). Trap handled: "45 past" → count down, not up.
- **Check (14–18):** hand on the 8, hour hand nearly at 6 → **20 to 6**. Wrong-answer playbook: "40 past" (no switch), "8 to" (number ≠ minutes), "20 to 5" (hour-hand-between-numbers trap).
- **Practice (18–22):** adaptive 10-item bank — quarter to / 10-jumps / half-past switch (level 1), read + SET 'to' times, next-hour naming, countdown ladder (level 2), stretch: past-vs-to sorting with no line drawn, '40 past' true/false, fix-a-friend's-reading (level 3).
- **Recap (22–24):** child states the rule ("after half past, count the minutes left to the next hour"); process praise for switching to the countdown.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach on the marked two-sided clock + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a set-the-clock, a next-hour and a mixed past/to item.

**Video (reinforce):** [How to Tell Time to the Nearest 5 Minutes | Reading a Clock — Math with Mr. J](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iJq0-FS6Ab4) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: shout "TO!" whenever the minute hand lands on the left side.

**Differentiation:** support — dashed middle line stays visible, count the remaining minutes together · stretch — mixed past/to faces, link to the daily timetable.
**Evidence:** reads 'minutes to' incl. quarter to, sets "twenty to 6", chooses past vs to correctly.
