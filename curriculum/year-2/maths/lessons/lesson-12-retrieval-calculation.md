## Flow (all AI, "Maths Gym Day" + train-track relay)

- **Hook (0–3):** gym-day energy — warm-up skip-count in 5s to 30; retrieval: why estimate first? Train-track relay introduced: every correct answer moves the train one station.
- **Explain (3–6, light — review lesson):** one modelled rep of the routine on 48 + 37 — ESTIMATE (~90), SOLVE (exchange → 85), CHECK. No new maths.
- **Example (6–9):** child runs the routine on 71 − 26 (= 45, break-a-ten), then the inverse superset: 6 × 5 = 30 → 30 ÷ 5 = 6.
- **Check (9–12):** 30 ÷ 10 = 3, plus the detective proof — which subtraction proves 48 + 37 = 85? (85 − 37 = 48.)
- **Practice (12–17):** the main workout — adaptive **11-item interleaved bank** deliberately alternating strands: warm-ups 23+14 / 2×5 / 10÷2 (level 1); 56+28, 64−39, 7×5, 25÷5, inverse-check MC (level 2); stretch: best-estimate MC, the two-step "5 bags of 5, eat 3" (22), and 70 − 23 crossing the ten (47). Misconceptions covered: forgotten exchanged ten, "713"-style digit gluing, ones flipped "because you can't do 4 − 9", ÷ read as −, skipping the estimate.
- **Recap (17–20):** honest self-assessment — strongest muscle and the one needing gym time (logged as the next warm-up); process praise ("you estimated before every answer").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (level 3 = timed streak, no tools) · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach + backup tool + level 1 · mastery = 6+ correct across ≥3 strands with estimates and one inverse check.

**Video:** none — a fluency workout, all retrieval (per the KB honesty rule, no invented links).

**Differentiation:** support — backup tools one tap away, fewer reps · stretch — timed streak, two-step problem.
**Evidence:** mixed calculation set completed fluently, estimating first, with an inverse check.
