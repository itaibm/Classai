## Flow (all AI, "break the ten" frame — Lesson 6's exchange, reversed)

- **Hook (0–3):** you had 63p, spent 28p — what's left? Retrieval: "yesterday we traded ten ones for a ten — what might we do today, the other way round?"
- **Explain (3–10):** animated whiteboard builds 63, hits "not enough ones" (3 vs 8), names and forbids the sneaky flip (8 − 3), then **breaks a ten**: 63 → 50 + 13; 13 − 8 = 5; 50 − 20 = 30 → **35**. Anchor: "Not enough ones? Break a ten."
- **Example (10–16):** shared 52 − 27 — child spots the shortage, renames 52 as 40 + 12, answers 25. Traps handled: "35" (flip / unreduced tens), "79" (added).
- **Check (16–20):** solo 63 − 28 → 35, closing the hook. Wrong-answer playbook: **"45"** (the KB smaller-from-larger flip, or tens not reduced) and "91" (added).
- **Practice (20–25):** adaptive 10-item bank — spot-the-shortage and renaming (level 1), 52−27 / 71−26 / 84−38 + a break-or-not trueFalse and categorize (level 2), stretch: 70 − 23 across a multiple of ten, "explain Mia's flip" with the add-back proof, renaming 63 = 50 + 13.
- **Recap (25–28):** child states the rule — break a ten when the ones run short; process praise ("you changed the ten, never the question").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → slow ten-breaking re-teach + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. one with and one without exchange.
