# Lesson 4 — Ordering numbers to 100
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 1 (Place value to 100)** · 22 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can put a set of two-digit numbers in order, smallest first."

## Flow (all AI, "book-fixer" mission)

- **Hook (0–3):** dropped book, muddled pages 52, 25, 50 — the story won't make sense until they're ordered. Retrieval: the golden rule from Lesson 3.
- **Explain (3–8):** the routine **"find the smallest, place it, repeat"** modeled on the whiteboard with a 0–100 track — including the equal-tens moment (50 before 52 because ones break the tie).
- **Example (8–13):** child runs the routine on 41, 14, 40 using the app's **drag-to-order block** — picks 14, then resolves the 40/41 tie.
- **Check (13–16):** solo ordering of the hook set; must justify why 50 < 52.
- **Practice (16–20):** adaptive 9-item bank — round tens and smallest-of-three (level 1); 13/31/33, four numbers with an equal-tens trio (26/60/62/66), **largest-first flip**, track positions (level 2); stretch: double-trap 78/80/87/88, "between 47 and 52 with 5 tens" riddle, and refereeing a friend's wrong order.
- **Recap (20–22):** child teaches the routine back; celebrates finishing the whole place-value unit.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → number-track re-teach + 2-number sets · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a 4-number equal-tens set and a largest-first flip → triggers the end-of-unit check.

**End-of-Unit 1 check (built in):** 38 = _ tens _ ones · partition 76 · bigger: 27 or 72 · order 52, 25, 50.

**Optional video (reinforce):** [Arrange the Numbers — TutWay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gBqbUf2_8aI) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: point where each number goes before the video places it.

**Differentiation:** support — every number on the track, two-number sets · stretch — 4+ numbers, both directions, riddles, referee mode.
**Evidence:** orders 3–4 numbers with tens-first justification incl. an equal-tens pair.
