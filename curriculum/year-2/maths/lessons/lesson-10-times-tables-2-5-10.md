# Lesson 10 — The 2, 5 and 10 times tables
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 2 (Calculation)** · 28 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can recall facts from the 2, 5 and 10 times tables."
*(Human view of [`lesson-10-times-tables-2-5-10.json`](./lesson-10-times-tables-2-5-10.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "collect the facts" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** count five 5p coins — 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 — "you just did a times-table fact in disguise! 5 × 5 = 25." Retrieval: what does 3 × 5 mean?
- **Explain (3–9):** number line builds the 5s jump by jump, each jump labelled with its fact ("a fact is just the skip-count, frozen"); pattern table — 2s are the doubles, 5s end in 0/5, 10s end in 0.
- **Example (9–15):** 4-by-5 emoji array counted in fives; child matches facts to totals (4×5, 4×10, 3×2, 6×10). Trap handled: × read as + → count the rows.
- **Check (15–18):** the commutativity diagnostic — "if 2 × 10 = 20, what is 10 × 2?" (instant 20, no recount) plus 5 × 5 with a "how would you check it fast?" probe.
- **Practice (18–25):** adaptive 11-item bank — guided jumps (level 1), quick-fire 6×5 / 4×10 / 7×2 / match-pairs / turn-around trueFalse (level 2), and stretch: derive 6×5 from 5×5, last-digit sorting for the 5s, 9×10, "convince a friend 8 × 2 is free." Facts the child works out get re-asked a minute later to convert into recall.
- **Recap (25–28):** child names one fact they now own; process praise ("you jumped on from facts you knew").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (level 3 = interleaved + timed) · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach + number-line skip-count + level 1 · mastery = 5+ correct incl. a commutative pair and a derived fact, two instant.

**Video (reinforce):** [The Counting by Fives Song | Counting Songs | Scratch Garden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EemjeA2Djjw) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: sing along in 5s — shout "5 × 5!" at 25.

**Differentiation:** support — number line stays out, one table at a time (10s first) · stretch — speed round, derive 8×5 / 9×10.
**Evidence:** recalls a spread of 2/5/10 facts incl. a commutative pair and one derived fact.
