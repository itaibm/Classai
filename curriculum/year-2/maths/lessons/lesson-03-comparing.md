# Lesson 3 — Comparing two-digit numbers
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 1 (Place value to 100)** · 24 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can compare two two-digit numbers by looking at the tens first."

## Flow (all AI)

- **Hook (0–3):** postman puzzle — houses 38 and 41, which does he reach first? Retrieval: tens in each.
- **Explain (3–9):** animated side-by-side **rod towers** — 4 tens vs 3 tens; the taller tower wins even though 38 has more ones. The **> hungry mouth** eats the bigger number. Anchor: *"Tens first."*
- **Example (9–14):** equal-tens case 47 vs 43 — the child discovers the fallback themselves: tens tie → ones break the tie.
- **Check (14–17):** the classic trap **19 vs 21**. Wrong-answer playbook: "19 because 9 > 1" → draw both towers.
- **Practice (17–22):** adaptive 10-item bank — far-apart tens and mouth-direction (level 1); 27 vs 72, equal tens 64 vs 61, across-a-ten 50 vs 49, symbol fill-ins (level 2); stretch: the trap restated (68 vs 71 true/false), greatest of three, "60 > 59 — by how much?", and "make a number that beats 55 and prove it."
- **Recap (22–24):** child teaches the golden rule back; process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → tower re-teach + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. an equal-tens pair and a trap item, recorded with > or <.

**Optional video (reinforce):** [Greater Than Less Than Song — NUMBEROCK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3qisu9NF1_0) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: point which way the mouth opens each time it appears.

**Differentiation:** support — towers drawn side by side, rule said aloud · stretch — three-number compares, by-how-much reasoning.
**Evidence:** compares 2–3 pairs with tens-first reasoning incl. an equal-tens pair, recorded with > or <.
