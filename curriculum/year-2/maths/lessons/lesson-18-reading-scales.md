# Lesson 18 — Reading scales: mass and capacity
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 5 (Measurement)** · 24 min · **Delivery: 🧑‍🏫 human intro → 🤖 AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can read a scale and choose the right unit for mass and capacity."
*(Human view of [`lesson-18-reading-scales.json`](./lesson-18-reading-scales.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

## Your part (first ~14 minutes, REAL jug + scales — includes the reveal pour)

**Lay out before the lesson:** kitchen scales, a marked measuring jug, a jug of coloured water, a tall thin glass and a short wide cup with the **same** capacity (test them first!), three fruits to weigh, a towel. Keep it all in reach — the child weighs and pours during practice.

- **Hook (0–3):** tall skinny glass vs short fat cup — "which holds more?" The child COMMITS to a guess (don't pour yet). Retrieval: ruler marks stood for amounts.
- **Explain (3–8):** the golden question — what is ONE mark worth? Pour to the second jug mark, touch-count "100… 200 — 200 ml". Weigh an apple; 1 kg = 1000 g, grams for light, kilograms for heavy.
- **Example / THE REVEAL (8–14):** tip the whole tall glass into the short cup — it fits, same water! Height doesn't decide. Child pours to the third mark and reads 300 ml.
- **Handoff line:** *"Now my friend on the screen has some scales for you to read. Watch!"* → the AI takes over.

## AI part

- **Check (14–18):** 1 kg vs 500 g (match units: 1000 g beats 500 g) + an animated jug read at the second mark → 200 ml. Wrong-answer playbook: "500 > 1", marks-counted-as-ones ("2 ml"), height-means-more relapse.
- **Practice (18–22):** weigh three fruits and order them; read jug levels; pick units. 9-item adaptive bank — 1 kg vs 500 g, strawberry in g or kg (level 1), interval reads (third mark = 300 ml, needle at 400 g), the pour trueFalse, g/kg sorting with the bag-of-sugar test (level 2), stretch: halfway between marks ≈ 150 ml, kilo = 1000, and "prove it to Ben" (measure, don't argue).
- **Recap (22–24):** the surprise retold + the golden question. Process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → reteach + real re-pour/re-weigh touching each mark + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. an interval read and a unit comparison.

**Optional video (reinforce, unverified — search the channel):** BBC Bitesize, search "measuring mass and capacity KS1". Task: call out what one mark is worth before the presenter does.

**Differentiation:** support — finger-on-mark counting, levels exactly on marks · stretch — between-marks estimates, 750 g vs 1 kg via 1000 g.
**Evidence:** reads scales by interval value, compares 1 kg vs 500 g, rejects height-means-more citing the pour.
