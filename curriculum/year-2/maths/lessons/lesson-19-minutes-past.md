# Lesson 19 — Minutes past the hour
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 6 (Time to 5 minutes)** · 22 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can read 'minutes past' times by counting the minute hand in 5s."
*(Human view of [`lesson-19-minutes-past.json`](./lesson-19-minutes-past.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you — a real teaching clock is optional, not required.)*

## Flow (all AI, "crack the clock code" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** "Every number on this clock is worth MORE than it looks — what's the 4 really worth?" Child guesses, then warms up the code key: counting in 5s.
- **Explain (3–8):** code cracked on the animated clock — the **LONG hand counts minutes, each number worth 5**. Sweep from the 12: 5 past at the 1… **quarter past** at the 3… and the whole right-hand side (12 round to 6) is "**past**". The 4 decoded: 5, 10, 15, 20 — twenty past. Anchor: long = minutes, short = hours. Whiteboard clock shows the orange 5s labels round the face.
- **Example (8–13):** child drives the clock — points the long hand at the 3 and counts the 5s aloud (quarter past), then reads the 2 (10 past).
- **Check (13–17):** the diagnostic — long hand on the 4: **20 past, or "4 minutes past"?** Plus "which hand were you reading?" Traps handled: number-read-as-minutes, hand mix-up, and counting the 12 as 5.
- **Practice (17–20):** adaptive 9-item bank (levels 1–3) — which hand counts minutes + the hand on the 1 (level 1), the 4-means-20 entry, quarter past, a where-points-to-time match and the o'clock hands question (level 2), and stretch: **25 past 7** with the hour read from the short hand, the 4-means-4 true/false (with the WHY: 60 ÷ 12 = 5), and "teach the code to a friend who says 'it's 2 minutes past'." Both directions: reading faces AND setting them.
- **Recap (20–22):** child states the code (each number = 5) and the 'past' side; cliffhanger for Lesson 20 — "something funny happens when the long hand goes PAST the 6…"

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach counting the 5s round the clock together, touching each number, + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. the 4-means-20 case and quarter past, with the 5s count explained at least once.

**Optional video (reinforce, unverified — search the channel):** BBC Bitesize, search "telling the time to 5 minutes KS1". Task: crack the code before the video does — shout the REAL minutes when the hand lands (the 3 means 15!).

**Differentiation:** support — count the 5s round to the number every time, stick to 5/10/15 · stretch — 20/25 past with the hour ("25 past 7", "7:25"), and explain WHY each number is worth 5.
**Evidence:** reads 'minutes past' times to 5 minutes incl. quarter past by counting in 5s, and picks the minute hand correctly when the hands could be confused.
