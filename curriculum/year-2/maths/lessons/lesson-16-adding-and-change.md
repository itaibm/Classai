# Lesson 16 — Adding amounts and finding change
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 4 (Money: pounds and pence)** · 22 min · **Delivery: 🧑‍🏫 human intro → 🤖 AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can total a small shopping list and work out change by counting up."
*(Human view of [`lesson-16-adding-and-change.json`](./lesson-16-adding-and-change.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

## Your part (first ~8 minutes, REAL shop + coins)

**Lay out before the lesson:** a pretend shop with price labels — pen 70p, rubber 30p, ruler 25p, toy 65p — plus plastic coins, a £1 and a £2 coin, and a mini-whiteboard. Keep the shop in reach; the child runs it during practice.

- **Hook (0–3):** "A pen costs 70p, you hand over £1 — do I keep the extra?" Play the shopkeeper, hand out. Retrieval: £1 = 100p.
- **Explain (3–8):** total 30p + 25p by counting on (30… 50… 55). Then change the star move: count UP from the price — 65 → 70 is 5p, 70 → 100 is 30p, so 35p change — laying a real coin per hop.
- **Handoff line:** *"Now my friend on the screen will draw those hops. Watch!"* → the AI takes over.

## AI part

- **Example (8–13):** animated number-line hops 65 → 70 → 100; child does the pen (70p from £1) — counts 80, 90, 100 → 30p. Traps handled: giving back the price, adding price + payment.
- **Check (13–17):** total 30p + 25p (= 55p, tens first) + change for the 70p pen. Wrong-answer playbook: dropped 5 (50p), tens muddle (65p), wrong-direction change.
- **Practice (17–20):** child runs the shop. 9-item adaptive bank — one-hop changes and simple totals (level 1), 65p-from-£1, "which way is right?", price↔change matching with the price+change=100 checker (level 2), stretch: two-step basket-then-change (45p), change from £2 for £1.20 (80p), and "Ava says 35p — check her".
- **Recap (20–22):** count UP, not back; first hop to the friendly ten. Process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → reteach laying a real coin per hop + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a total-over-ten and a change-from-£1.

**No video** — the money video lives in Lesson 15; this lesson is hands-on shop play.

**Differentiation:** support — coin-per-hop count-ups, one-hop changes (90p/95p) · stretch — change from £2, checking the tutor's wrong change.
**Evidence:** totals two amounts across a ten and finds change from £1 by counting up.
