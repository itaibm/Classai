# Lesson 1 — Tens and ones to 100
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 1 (Place value to 100)** · 22 min · **Delivery: 👤 human intro → 🤖 AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can build any number to 100 and say how many tens and ones it has."
*(Human view of [`lesson-01-tens-and-ones.json`](./lesson-01-tens-and-ones.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

## Your part (first ~8 minutes, real rods & cubes)

**Lay out before the lesson:** base-10 rods and cubes to 100, place-value mat (Tens | Ones), mini-whiteboard with **34** and **43** written large. Keep rods within the child's reach afterwards — the AI will ask the child to build during practice.

1. **Hook (0–3):** "34 and 43 use exactly the same digits — are they the same number? Which would you rather have, 34 sweets or 43?" Don't resolve it. Retrieve: "Which digit tells the tens?" *(If shaky: hand them one rod + one cube, count the rod in tens.)*
2. **Explain (3–8):** Build 65 aloud — "Six tens: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60. Five ones: 61…65. The 6 isn't six — it's six TENS, sixty. **The place tells the value.**"
3. **Handoff line (say as written):** *"Now my friend on the screen is going to draw what we just built. Watch!"* → AI takes over.

## AI part (8–22 min)

- **Example (8–12):** character redraws 65 as sticks & dots on the animated whiteboard, then the child directs the drawing of 24 ("How many sticks? How many dots?").
- **Check (12–15):** child builds **47** with real rods, answers on screen. Traps handled: "7 tens and 4 ones" (columns swapped) and "4 and 7" (digits without values).
- **Practice (15–20):** adaptive, from a 10-item bank (levels 1–3) — includes the sneaky **70** (zero ones), reversed items ("5 tens and 3 ones — what number?"), the 34-vs-43 true/false, biggest-number-from-digits, and a 3-tens vs 3-ones sort. Every item has hints, wrong-answer diagnosis, and a scripted re-teach.
- **Recap (20–22):** child states the rule ("place changes value"); process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · two right → level up · two misses on a skill → re-teach + real rods + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. the zero-ones case. Always end on an earned win.

**Optional video (reinforce, after the lesson):** [Place Value Blocks (2-Digit Numbers) — Elementary Math with Mr. J](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZQ3X93tjMYM) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Watching task: shout "TEN!" at every ten-block, count along in tens. After: "How did Mr. J count the ten-blocks?" → in tens.

**Differentiation:** support — labelled mat, teen numbers first · stretch — no-equipment questions, 9-and-2 biggest number.
**Evidence:** builds 2–3 numbers and names tens/ones, including a number ending in 0.
