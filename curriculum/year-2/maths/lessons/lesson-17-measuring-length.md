# Lesson 17 — Measuring length with a ruler
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 5 (Measurement)** · 22 min · **Delivery: 🧑‍🏫 human intro → 🤖 AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can measure a length in cm by lining the object up with zero."
*(Human view of [`lesson-17-measuring-length.json`](./lesson-17-measuring-length.json) — the JSON is the source of truth.)*

## Your part (first ~8 minutes, REAL ruler + objects)

**Lay out before the lesson:** cm rulers, a tape measure (and metre stick if you have one), four objects to measure — pencil, book, crayon, ribbon — sticky notes, and a paper strip on the wall for a height mark. Keep everything in reach; the child measures real objects during practice.

- **Hook (0–3):** "How tall are you in centimetres?" — the child guesses, you write it on a sticky note (checked in the extension). Retrieval: counting starts at 0.
- **Explain (3–8):** the golden rule — pencil's end EXACTLY on the 0, read the far end: 14 cm. Then deliberately be "lazy": start at 1 and the far end reads 15 — but the pencil hasn't grown! End on zero, every time.
- **Handoff line:** *"Now my friend on the screen will draw a giant ruler. Watch!"* → the AI takes over.

## AI part

- **Example (8–13):** animated giant ruler with a crayon from 0 to 9 — the child inspects the zero, reads 9 cm, then copies the measure with the real crayon.
- **Check (13–16):** the diagnostic — object from 0 to 15 → **15 cm**. Wrong-answer playbook: 14 (off-by-one/start-at-1), 0 (read the near end), 16 (counted ticks).
- **Practice (16–20):** measure all four real objects, record on sticky notes, order shortest → longest. 9-item adaptive bank — golden rule + read-from-0 (level 1), the 0-to-15 read, Leo's start-at-1 trap (13 read = 12 real), cm-vs-m, ordering (level 2), stretch: string from 2 to 11 (= 9 cm gap), 30 cm vs 1 m, and "Mia measures from the ruler's edge — what's wrong?"
- **Recap (20–22):** child states the golden rule; height guess teed up for the extension. Process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → reteach + real re-measure with a finger on the 0 + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. an off-by-one trap and a real zero-aligned measure.

**Optional video (reinforce):** [How to Measure Different Objects in Centimetres using a Ruler! — Maths with Mum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJW7nE76Aok) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: be the zero inspector — shout "ZERO!" when the object's end sits on it.

**Differentiation:** support — start-at-1 demo side by side, objects pre-placed at 0 · stretch — estimate-then-measure, the 2-to-11 gap problem.
**Evidence:** aligns to 0 unprompted, reads cm accurately incl. 0-to-15, orders by length.
