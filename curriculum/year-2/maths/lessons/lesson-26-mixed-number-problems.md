# Lesson 26 — Mixed problems: number and calculation
**Maths · Year 2 · Unit 8 (Year-end consolidation)** · 24 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI** · **Kind: review**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can choose the right operation to solve a worded number problem."

## Flow (all AI, maths-detective frame — the hard part is CHOOSING the operation)

- **Hook (0–3):** "Put on your detective hat — every word problem hides one clue: WHICH operation?" Retrieval: estimate before solving.
- **Explain (3–8):** three cases cracked aloud — "give away" → subtract (48 − 19 ≈ 30 → 29), "bags of" → multiply (5 × 5 = 25), "share between" → divide (20 ÷ 4 = 5). Clue-word table stays on screen. Routine: estimate → solve → check.
- **Example (8–13):** child spots the clue in "5 bags of 5 sweets" and names multiply BEFORE solving; counts in fives to 25. Add-trap (5 + 5 = 10) handled by acting the story out.
- **Check (13–17):** solve the bags case fully → 25, justify the clue. Wrong-answer playbook: 10 (added), 30 (one group too far).
- **Practice (17–21):** adaptive 12-item bank, one of each operation before repeats — resurfaces the year's traps: 48 − 19 digit-swap (→31), 48 + 37 dropped carry (→75/715), the 19 vs 21 tens-first trap, sharing vs subtracting, inverse checking, estimating; stretch: two-step change problem (3 pens at 5p from 20p → 5p), write-your-own ÷ story, sort six stories by operation.
- **Recap (21–24):** child states their rule — find the clue, act it out, bigger or smaller? Process praise: "you acted the story out before choosing."

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (interleave ops at 3) · 2 misses → act-it-out reteach + level 1 · mastery = 4+ correct over 3+ operations incl. one exchange/regroup with an estimate.

**No video** — consolidation lesson; the minutes go to cases.

**Differentiation:** support — act out with counters, clue card visible, one-step only · stretch — two-step problems, author a story for 20 ÷ 4.
**Evidence:** correct operation chosen for mixed worded problems, solved with estimate and check.
