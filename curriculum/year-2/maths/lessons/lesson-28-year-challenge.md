## Flow (all AI, "champion circuit" frame — celebration of mastery, not a test)

- **Hook (0–3):** "The Year 2 Maths Champion Challenge — six stations, one lap, a badge at the finish!" Child names their favourite-looking station.
- **Explain (3–6):** the champion routine modelled at the place-value station (76 = 70 + 6): read → solve → flip the answer and SELF-CHECK → tick or retry with a smaller step.
- **Example (6–9):** times-table station together — 5 × 5 = 25 with the self-check voiced ("twenty-five — checked, correct!"). Then the child goes solo.
- **Check (9–13):** fraction station — ¾ of 12 → 9 (share into 4, count 3 plates). Traps handled: 8 (subtracted), 3 (one quarter only), skipping the self-check.
- **Practice (13–20):** the full solo circuit from a 14-item bank spanning every strand — partition 76, 48 + 37 = 85 (dropped-carry trap), 71 − 26 = 45 (digit-swap trap), ×5/×10 facts, ¼ of 8 and ¾ of 12, 70p pen change from £1 → 30p, quarter past 3 = 3:15 and quarter to 7 = 6:45 (past/to trap), chart "most" (tallest bar), 72 vs 27 (tens first), unequal-halves true/false; bonus: design-your-own station. Missed stations are retried with a smaller step so the lap ends complete.
- **Recap (20–24):** champion's interview — easiest station, one to practise, one proud thing from the year — then the badge ceremony. The shaky strand is logged for the Year 3 handover.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · strands always rotate · 2 misses at a station → manipulative-on-screen reteach + level-1 retry so every station earns its tick · **masterySignal: self-checked correct answers across ≥6 strands at level 2+ → year-end mastery ≥ 0.8 for Y2 maths, secure vs shaky strands logged against the end-of-year mastery checks.**

**No video** — the whole session is the circuit.

**Differentiation:** support — child picks station order, pictures stay on screen · stretch — beat-the-clock lap, design-a-question-and-mark-the-tutor.
**Evidence:** a completed mixed circuit across all eight units, self-checked, with reflection — the Year 3 handover record.
