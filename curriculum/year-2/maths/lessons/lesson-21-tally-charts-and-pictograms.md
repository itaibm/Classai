## Flow (all AI, ice-cream survey mission; pictograms rendered as emoji rows with a key)

- **Hook (0–3):** "What's the family's favourite ice-cream flavour? We'll PROVE the winner with a chart." Child votes first. Retrieval: counting in 5s.
- **Explain (3–8):** whiteboard draws the tally **gate of five** stroke by stroke (fifth crosses — snap!); then the pictogram plot twist: key 🍦 = 2, so three ice creams = 2, 4, 6 — SIX. Anchor: "Read the key first."
- **Example (8–13):** child tallies 7 (one gate + 2) and reads the survey pictogram: key = 2, 4 symbols → 8. Trap handled: symbols counted as 1 → back to the key.
- **Check (13–17):** key 🍎 = 5, row of 4 apples → **20**. Wrong-answer playbook: "4" (key ignored), "9" (added 4 + 5 instead of counting 5s).
- **Practice (17–20):** adaptive 10-item bank — gate counting and key-first habit (level 1), keys of 2 and 5, building tallies for 12, tally↔total matching (level 2), stretch: half-symbol worth half the key, "every picture = 1" true/false, fix-a-friend's misread (level 3).
- **Recap (20–22):** winner crowned with real data; child states both habits — key first, fifth stroke crosses.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach + count one symbol's worth on fingers + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a tally item and a key ≥ 2 pictogram.

**Video:** none — the animated tally and emoji pictograms carry it (per the KB honesty rule, no unverified link).

**Differentiation:** support — key = 2 only, finger on each symbol · stretch — half symbols, design a pictogram and choose the key.
**Evidence:** tallies with gates; reads key-2 and key-5 pictograms by multiplying, not counting symbols.
