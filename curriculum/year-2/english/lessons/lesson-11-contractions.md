# Lesson 11 — Apostrophes job 1: squashing words (contractions)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 3 (Apostrophes & homophones)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can write a contraction with the apostrophe where letters are missing."
*(Human view of [`lesson-11-contractions.json`](./lesson-11-contractions.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "gravestone for the buried letters" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** "When we talk fast, we squash words — *do not* → *don't*. Letters fall out; the apostrophe is the little gravestone marking the gap." Child says a natural *don't* sentence.
- **Explain (3–8):** the three-step move on *don't, I'm, it's*: write both words → cross the dropped letter in red → apostrophe exactly in the gap.
- **Example (8–12):** shared squash of *can not, she will, we are*; matchPairs contraction ↔ long form. Probe: "in *can't*, which letters did we bury?" (the *n-o* of *not*).
- **Check (12–14):** contract *cannot* — multipleChoice with both classic traps as options: *ca'nt* (apostrophe at the join) and *cant* (no gravestone).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 9-item bank — *I'm*/*don't* fills (level 1), matchPairs, *we're* vs *were*, "which letters went missing in *she'll*?" (level 2), write-your-own sentence, fix a friend's *do'nt*, and *they are → they're* with there/their decoys pre-seeding Lesson 14 (level 3).
- **Recap (18–20):** child states job 1 and where the apostrophe sits; process praise; teaser for job 2 (belonging).

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → slow-motion-squash reteach + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a placement question and a written contraction.

**Video (reinforce):** [Sing with Grammarsaurus - Apostrophes — Grammarsaurus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Ov54DSvjS8) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: shout the missing letters before the apostrophe appears.

**Differentiation:** support — dropped letters pre-crossed, child places the apostrophe · stretch — *they're*/*you're* naming the missing *a*.
**Evidence:** 3+ contractions with the apostrophe placed where letters dropped, buried letters named.
