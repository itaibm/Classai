## Flow (all AI, "grow a tail" frame — first lesson of Unit 2)

- **Hook (0–3):** *jump* grows a tail and becomes *jumping* — animated whiteboard slide-on. Retrieval: Year 1 plural (*cat → cats*).
- **Explain (3–8):** **root** = main word, **suffix** = the tail. Today's easy rule: **just add it on — nothing inside the root changes.** Two-colour table: jump/play × -ing/-ed, root always visible and untouched.
- **Example (8–12):** child builds *helping, painting, kicked* via fillBlank word banks seeded with the real traps (*helpping, paining, kickt*).
- **Check (12–14):** "Add -ing to *play*" → *playing*, then read it back aloud (speak block). Wrong-answer playbook: *plaing/playling* (changed the root) and *played* (wrong tail).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 9-item bank — *jumped* + find-the-root (level 1), *kicking, painted*, root+tail matching, and a spoken read-back of all four builds (level 2), *faster/fastest* and "a friend wrote *plaing* — what went wrong?" (level 3). Covers changing-the-root, tail mix-ups, and sound-spelling traps (*jumpt, fastist*).
- **Recap (18–20):** child defines root and suffix and states the rule; teaser for Lesson 6's hopping-off silent *e*.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → re-teach with the root visible + root pre-written · read-backs spoken aloud.
