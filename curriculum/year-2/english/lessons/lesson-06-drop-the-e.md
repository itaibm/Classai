# Lesson 6 — Drop the e (hope → hoping)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 2 (Spelling with suffixes)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can drop a silent e before adding a vowel suffix."
*(Human view of [`lesson-06-drop-the-e.json`](./lesson-06-drop-the-e.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "kick the e off" game frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** watch the spelling go WRONG — "hopeing", read out loud all clunky — then the silent e HOPS off to fix it. Retrieval: jump + -ing (Lesson 5's just-add).
- **Explain (3–8):** animated whiteboard crosses the e in red, then adds the tail: hope → hoping, bake → baking / baked. Repeated phrase: **"Drop the e before a vowel suffix."**
- **Example (8–12):** child is the "e-kicker" — smiling, riding, nicer — naming the move (cross it off) before spelling.
- **Check (12–14):** hope + -ed → **hoped**. Wrong-answer playbook: *hopeed* (kept the e), *hopped* (wrong rule — doubling, seeded for Lesson 7), *hopd* (lost the suffix's e).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 9-item bank — baking/smiling with word banks + read *hoping* aloud (level 1), hoped/racing/match-the-roots (level 2), stretch: nicer/largest (-er/-est) and "a friend wrote *hopeing* — what went wrong?" (level 3).
- **Recap (18–20):** child teaches the rule back + says it aloud like a champion; process praise; teaser for hop vs hope.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with the e crossed in red + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct across levels 2–3 incl. an -ed and an -er/-est form.

**Misconceptions covered:** *hopeing/bakeing* (kept the e) · *hopped* for *hoped* (doubled instead of dropped) · reading the built word wrong (*hoping* vs *hopping*).

**Differentiation:** support — tutor crosses the e, child adds the tail · stretch — nicer/largest + explain why the e goes.
**Evidence:** drops the e correctly in 3 of 4 words (hoping, baked, smiling) and reads each back.
