## Flow (all AI, "Robot vs Storyteller face-off" — this is the End-of-Unit 1 check)

- **Hook (0–3):** the face-off rules — robot read first, then beat it with the smooth read; you always win the second time. Warm-up speak block: *playground*.
- **Explain (3–6):** brisk refresh of both winning moves — chop the big words (clap/dot → read beats → push together) and re-read for the smooth win (crack-read → storyteller read, marks boss the voice).
- **Example (6–9):** together — beat-read *elephant*, then robot-and-storyteller read of "Sam ran to the park." Quick probe: what does the voice do at "He saw a huge dog!"?
- **Check (9–11):** "Read *playground* and tell me its beats" → play-ground (2). Wrong-answer playbook: one-gulp stall (clap first) and merged beats (chin-drop trick).
- **Practice (11–17):** the evidence round from a 9-item speak-heavy bank — beat-read *picnic, elephant, stegosaurus* (+ stretch *caterpillar*), question-lift speak item, beat-count sort (2/3/4 beats), the full 5-sentence passage storyteller read, and a teach-the-robot strategy check. Optional timer for smoothness, never speed.
- **Recap (17–20):** child teaches back both winning moves; end-of-unit process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → scripted re-teach + shorter word/sentence · bias to **speak** items — the review IS the child reading aloud.
**Mastery signal = End-of-Unit 1 standard:** three long words read by beats + passage read smoothly with some expression, not word-by-word.

**Video:** none — retrieval lesson, every minute is the child reading. (Unit 1's verified video is on Lesson 1.)

**Differentiation:** support — two cards + 2–3 sentence passage, shared decode read · stretch — timed smoothness + *caterpillar*.
**Evidence:** child beat-reads three unfamiliar long words and reads the passage on a fluency pass with some expression.
