# Lesson 17 — Expanded noun phrases (paint a picture)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 4 (Word classes & sentence types)** · 25 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can expand a noun with adjectives, using a comma between two."
*(Human view of [`lesson-17-expanded-noun-phrases.json`](./lesson-17-expanded-noun-phrases.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "paint a picture with words" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** close your eyes — *a dog* could be ANY dog; *a small, noisy dog* you can really SEE. Retrieval: is *fluffy* a noun, verb or adjective?
- **Explain (3–9):** grow a noun in three stages on the animated whiteboard: *castle → a dark castle → a dark, spooky castle* — the comma appears in bold at stage three. Rule landed: **a comma between two adjectives**. Names the result: an *expanded noun phrase*.
- **Example (9–14):** shared build of *a dark, silent forest* (fillBlank from the word-bank) and *a slimy, green monster*; child states where the comma goes and why.
- **Check (14–17):** punctuation inspector — pick the correct version of the spooky castle phrase. Traps: no comma (*a dark spooky castle*), comma after *a*.
- **Practice (17–22):** adaptive 9-item bank — one adjective + comma-only fills (level 1); complete/spot/write phrases and an adjectives-only sort (level 2); stretch: rebuild a jumbled sentence around the phrase (ordering), write a full sentence with an ENP, and edit *a big red fast car* (level 3).
- **Recap (22–25):** child states the grow-a-noun stages + comma rule; process praise quoting their own best phrase.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with the three-stage build + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a written phrase with a correct comma and a spot-the-error.

**Optional video (reinforce, unverified spec):** search **Grammarsaurus** for *"expanded noun phrases"*. Task: a finger per adjective; shout "COMMA!" when two describers need one.

**Differentiation:** support — word-bank + tutor supplies noun and first adjective · stretch — own adjectives, phrase inside a full sentence.
**Evidence:** two+ ENPs each with two comma-separated adjectives; fixes a missing-comma phrase.
