**Objective:** "By the end, I can join two ideas with the right conjunction, including *because* for a reason."

## Flow (all AI, "the magic word because" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** *I was happy.* …Why, though? Add **because** and tell the reason. Child finishes "I was happy because…" their own way; two tiny dog sentences banked for later.
- **Explain (3–9):** two conjunction families on the anchor table — *and* adds / *but* difference / *or* choice (equal ideas); *because* reason / *when* time / *if* maybe (extra part). *Because* + reason underlined: **the reason comes straight after because**.
- **Example (9–14):** shared joins — rain + stayed in → *because*; apple + pear → *or*; the child's own dog sentences joined. Meaning question drilled: add? difference? choice? reason?
- **Check (14–18):** *I was tired ___ I went to bed.* → *because*. Traps handled: "and" (defaulting), "but" (contrast-for-cause). Probe: what does *because* tell the reader?
- **Practice (18–23):** adaptive 9-item bank — *and* joins + spot-the-reason-word (level 1); *but*/*or* fills, "I was late because…" with a REAL reason (shortText), word→meaning matchPairs (level 2); stretch: *when/if*, **clause-order detective** (*It was cold because I put on my coat* = magic coat!), and rebuild a jumbled because sentence (ordering) (level 3).
- **Recap (23–25):** child produces a because sentence with a genuine reason; process praise ("you stopped reaching for *and*").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with bank meanings + level 1 (and vs because) · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a reasoned, right-order *because*.
