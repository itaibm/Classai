# Lesson 2 — Dinosaur names & place names: beat-reading challenge
**English · Year 2 · Unit 1 (Fluent reading: longer words & expression)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can read very long, unfamiliar words by syllable-chopping."
*(Human view of [`lesson-02-dinosaur-names.json`](./lesson-02-dinosaur-names.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "stepping stones" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** "Could you read a word as long as a dinosaur is tall? *Ste-go-sau-rus!*" Warm-up speak block: *elephant* from Lesson 1.
- **Explain (3–8):** the new tool — **dot the beats before you read** — modelled on *triceratops* with an animated whiteboard (four dots appear one by one). Image: dots are stepping stones across a big puddle.
- **Example (8–12):** shared dotting of *Manchester* (Man·chest·er) and *T-rex*; child leads on *triceratops* via a speak block.
- **Check (12–14):** "Read *stegosaurus* by its beats — dot them first." Wrong-answer playbook: one-blur overload (dot together) and beats-read-but-not-blended (join the halves: ste-go… sau-rus).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 9-item speak-heavy bank — *T-rex, London* (level 1), *Manchester, stegosaurus, diplodocus* beat counting + match-the-stones (level 2), *diplodocus, Australia*, and dot-it-yourself *triceratops* (level 3). Covers guessing-from-first-letter and losing-the-thread-while-blending.
- **Recap (18–20):** child states the trick (dot, read each beat, push together); process praise ("you didn't guess").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (level 3 = dot the beats yourself) · 2 misses → scripted re-teach + drop to a 2-beat word · bias to **speak** items.

**Video:** none — the animated whiteboard dots carry the visual teaching. (Unit 1's verified video is on Lesson 1.)

**Differentiation:** support — pre-dotted beats, start with *T-rex* · stretch — no dots given, child marks them (*triceratops, Australia*).
**Evidence:** child independently dots and reads one unfamiliar 3+ beat word beat by beat, then blends it.
