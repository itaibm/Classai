## Flow (all AI, retrieval lesson closing the unit)

- **Hook (0–3):** the tongue-twister on the whiteboard — "*Two* biscuits is *too* many *to* eat!" — plus spaced warm-up: which of yesterday's three means "they are"? (*they're*).
- **Explain (3–8):** brisk meanings + tricks on a table — *two* = the number (the *w*, like *twin/twice*), *too* = also/very (**extra o for extra**), *to* = the everyday one; *here/hear* (**ear** inside) and *see/sea*.
- **Example (8–12):** shared meaning-first choices — "I have ___ cats" (*two*), "Can I come ___?" (*too*), one *hear* example.
- **Check (12–14):** "'I want to come ___' (also)" — multipleChoice; probe for the extra-o trick; *to*/*two* wrong paths scripted.
- **Practice (14–18):** **interleaved 11-item bank spanning the whole unit** — *two/too/to* gaps (level 1), *hear/sea*, a *their* retrieval, a *don't* contraction flashback, the *fresh egg's* trap as trueFalse (level 2), and stretch: the grand categorize sort (squash / belonging / plural), the double *too, to* gap, write-your-own *two-too-to* sentence (level 3). Director never runs the same skill twice in a row.
- **Recap (18–20):** child teaches back the three "too"-sounding words; unit closed with all four tools named; process praise.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (level 3 forces full interleaving) · 2 misses on a skill → cue-card reteach + level-1 same-skill item · mastery = 5+ correct incl. a *to/too/two*, a *there/their/they're* and an apostrophe retrieval.
