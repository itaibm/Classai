# Lesson 25 — End-of-year review: read it, prove it, write it
**English · Year 2 · Unit 5 (Comprehension & composition) · REVIEW** · 25 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can read fluently, infer with evidence, and write a correctly punctuated sentence."

## Flow (all AI, "show-off day" frame — low-stakes personal-best challenge across the whole year)

- **Hook (0–3):** show-off day — the whole year in one mini challenge, child vs their own personal best. Retrieval: how do we read impossibly long words? (chop into beats).
- **Explain (3–6):** the three stations modelled once each — **READ IT** (steg-o-saur-us → storyteller read), **PROVE IT** ("I think… because the text says…"), **WRITE IT** (capital + because reason + end mark).
- **Example (6–9):** shared warm-up — chop *playground*, read "The playground was silent under the falling snow" like a storyteller (speak block), recall the proof stem. Traps handled: stem without the evidence half; "because I know it".
- **Check (9–13):** the review passage **"The Lost Kite"** (richText, 5 sentences) — fluent read, then one evidenced inference: how does Ella feel when the string slips? Wrong-answer playbook: clue-free "sad"; "happy, because she got a kite" (guess, not text).
- **Practice (13–22):** the mixed solo round — director interleaves the 12-item bank across all five units, never two of the same strand back-to-back; the written because-sentence (l25-p09) appears for every child.
- **Recap (22–25):** growth reflection — one thing you can do now that you couldn't at the start of Year 2; year-end process praise ("you chop, you prove, you punctuate"). Take a bow.

**Misconceptions targeted (one last pass):** answering from memory/opinion instead of the text; inference without pointing to a clue; hoping/hopping and happyer suffix muddles; apostrophes on plain plurals (apple's 50p); instructions written as a story.

**Practice bank:** 12 items interleaving the whole year — L1 retrieval vs inference label, Sam's bag apostrophe, don't contraction, command spotting (4) · L2 hop+ing doubling, happy+er y→i, order the letter-posting steps, evidenced inference on Dev and the biscuit (4) · L3 **the written because-sentence** (capital + real reason + end mark), hoping/hopping meaning-and-why stretch, the grocer's-apostrophe trueFalse trap, four-way sentence-type sort (4). Every item: wrong answers diagnosed, hints, scripted reteach.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up (level 3 interleaves strands hard) · 2 misses on a strand → that item's reteach + a level-1 item from the same strand · mastery = fluent read + one evidenced inference + one written capital/because/end-mark sentence + 3+ correct mixed items → year-end mastery snapshot seeding the Year 3 baseline.

**Video:** none — review day; the child's own reading, proving and writing carry the lesson (Unit 5's verified clip lives in Lesson 23).

**Differentiation:** support — warm-up sentence instead of the full passage, stem card + capital/end-mark reminders on screen, two options not four · stretch — expanded noun phrase in the written sentence; prove Ella's SECOND feeling (relief at the flash of red) with its own clue.
**Extension:** a two-sentence book recommendation with a because reason and full punctuation — first Year 3-style opinion writing.
**Evidence:** a fluent passage read, one stem-and-clue inference, one correctly punctuated because-sentence, plus mixed-item accuracy across spelling, apostrophes and sentence types.
