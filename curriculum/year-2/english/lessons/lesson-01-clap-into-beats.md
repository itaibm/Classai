# Lesson 1 — Clap it into beats: reading long words by syllable
**English · Year 2 · Unit 1 (Fluent reading: longer words & expression)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can read a long word by chopping it into syllables (beats)."
*(Human view of [`lesson-01-clap-into-beats.json`](./lesson-01-clap-into-beats.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "knock the tower down" game frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** the scary word *fantastic* toppled clap by clap; warm-up speak block — child reads *cat* aloud (Year 1 blending retrieval).
- **Explain (3–8):** the three magic steps on *rabbit* — **clap the beats, read each beat, push together** — with animated whiteboard dots under *rab* and *bit*. Magic phrase: "Clap it into beats first."
- **Example (8–12):** clap-along on *sunset* and *playground*; child leads on *elephant* (3 beats) via a speak block.
- **Check (12–14):** "How many syllables in *elephant* — and read it." Wrong-answer playbook: merged beats (chin-drop trick) and one-gulp stalls (clap first).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 9-item bank, speak-heavy — the AI listens to the child beat-read *rabbit, picnic, basket, playground* (levels 1–2) up to *dinosaur, caterpillar, fantastic* (level 3), plus clap-and-sort beat counting. Covers the two misconceptions: guessing from the first letter, and one-gulp overload.
- **Recap (18–20):** child teaches back the three steps; process praise ("you clapped, you read every beat, no guessing").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach (clap together, dot the beats) + drop a level · bias to **speak** items — the tutor should hear the child read on most turns.

**Curated video (reinforce):** [#BacktoSchool - Introducing: Syllables | Learn to Read | Alphablocks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pIKzbTK7w-4) — Alphablocks (official) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: clap and say each word's beats BEFORE the video does. After-check: "What do we call each beat in a word?" → a syllable.

**Differentiation:** support — tutor claps first, 2-beat words only · stretch — 4-beat *caterpillar*.
**Evidence:** child claps and reads two long words (one 3-beat) with clap → read beats → blend, no one-gulp attempts.
