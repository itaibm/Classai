# Lesson 8 — y → i (happy → happier)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 2 (Spelling with suffixes)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can change a final y to i before -er/-est/-ed (but keep y before -ing)."

## Flow (all AI, "the y transforms" frame — two tricks in one lesson)

- **Hook (0–3):** *happyer*? Nope — watch the y TRANSFORM into an i: *happier*. Plus the twist preview: *crying* keeps its y. Retrieval: hope + -ing (drop the e).
- **Explain (3–8):** the two-part rule on the whiteboard: **y → i before -er/-est/-ed** (happy → happier/happiest, cry → cried) — **but keep the y before -ing** (crying, never *criing* — English hates ii).
- **Example (8–12):** child transforms funnier, sunniest, tried… then the twist: *trying* (y stays).
- **Check (12–14):** happy + -est → **happiest**. Wrong-answer playbook: *happyest* (kept the y), *happyiest* (i added without removing the y), *happist* (lost letters in the swap).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 10-item bank — funnier/happiest/true-false (level 1), sunniest, tried, **flying** (the exception) + match-the-builds (level 2), stretch: **sadder** (imposter — no y, it's a doubler!), reject *criing*, explain WHY crying keeps its y (level 3).
- **Recap (18–20):** child teaches back rule + twist aloud; all four suffix tricks now learned — Sorting Office teased for Lesson 9.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with the pair side by side (happy → happi__) + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct across levels 2–3 incl. an -ing exception and the sad decoy.

**Misconceptions covered:** *happyer/cryed* (kept the y) · *criing/fliing* (over-applied before -ing) · *sadier* (y rule where none applies — sad doubles).

**Differentiation:** support — copy-the-change pairs, exception delayed · stretch — explain the ii reason + trying, drier, busiest.
**Evidence:** changes y → i in 3+ words (happiest, cried, sadder) and keeps the y in flying/crying.
