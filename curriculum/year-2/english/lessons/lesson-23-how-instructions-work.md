# Lesson 23 — How instructions work (commands & order words)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 5 (Comprehension & composition)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can identify the features of instructions and order steps correctly."
*(Human view of [`lesson-23-how-instructions-work.json`](./lesson-23-how-instructions-work.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "instruction inspector" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** "Could your family make a jam sandwich from ONLY your writing?" — muddled steps on screen, mimed disastrously (jam before bread!). Retrieval: the bossy sentence = a **command**.
- **Explain (3–8):** feature tour on a real model sheet ("How to make a jam sandwich"): **title → you will need → numbered steps**, each starting with a **bossy verb** (*Get, Spread, Put, Cut*), marched along by **order words** (*first, next, then, finally*). One contrast: "Spread the jam." vs story-mode "I spread the jam."
- **Example (8–12):** shared unscramble (ordering block) with the fixing question — "could I really do this step yet?" — then read back with order words. Check: what sentence type starts each step?
- **Check (12–16):** spot-the-imposter MC — "I put the jam on the bread." caught as story-writing, not a command; child names a bossy verb.
- **Practice (16–19):** Chief Inspector drills from the 9-item bank.
- **Recap (19–20):** two features named aloud (speak block); tease next lesson — YOU write your own.

**Misconceptions targeted:** steps written as a story/statement ("Then I poured…"); illogical order (steps read alone, not as a sequence); order words as decoration; missing "you will need".

**Practice bank:** 9 items — L1 obey-test a command, order-word fillBlank (2) · L2 order teeth-brushing, find the bossy verb, command-vs-statement categorize (incl. the "I washed my hands" trap), features MC (4) · L3 fix a story-step into a command, spot the missing squash step, 5-step seed-planting ordering (3). Every item: wrong answers diagnosed, hints, scripted reteach.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → act-it-out reteach + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a full ordering and a story-step catch.

**Video (reinforce, ✅ verified):** [English Bossy Verbs Lesson Introduction — MrH teaches](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2fsyoMLOgd0) *(oEmbed-verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: shout every bossy verb back at the screen in your bossiest voice.

**Differentiation:** support — 3-step sets, read each step aloud before placing · stretch — spot the missing step, improve a vague one.
**Evidence:** orders a jumbled set correctly, identifies commands + bossy verbs, rejects a story-mode step.
