## Flow (all AI, "belonging labels" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** "Whose pencil is this? Add a tiny 's and the word TELLS you: *Mia's pencil*." Retrieval of Job 1: contract *do not*.
- **Explain (3–8):** the owner rule on a visible flip-table: *the tail of the cat → the cat's tail*, *Sam's bag*, *the dog's bone*. The move: find the OWNER → add 's to the owner.
- **Example (8–12):** shared flips — *the dog's bone* (fillBlank) and *the tutor's desk*, owner named first every time.
- **Check (12–14):** "show the bag belongs to Sam" — multipleChoice with both traps: *Sams bag* (no apostrophe) and *Sam bag's* ('s on the thing).
- **Practice (14–18):** adaptive 9-item bank — *Mia's pencil*, *the cat's tail* (level 1), flips, matchPairs long-form ↔ label, real belonging labels for home (level 2), and stretch: Job 1 vs Job 2 stretch-test (*Ben's kite* vs *it's raining*), write-your-own sentence, "what does *Kim's coat* mean?" (level 3).
- **Recap (18–20):** child states Job 2 + owner rule; process praise; teaser: "some apostrophes out there are FAKES" (Lesson 13).

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → three-step-flip reteach (owner → owner+'s → thing) + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. one flip and one own-written label.

**Video:** none — the flip-table and label-making carry the visuals.

**Differentiation:** support — owner pre-underlined, child adds 's · stretch — full sentence with a possessive.
**Evidence:** 3+ possessive phrases with 's on the owner.
