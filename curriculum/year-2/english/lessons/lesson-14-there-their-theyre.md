## Flow (all AI, "beat the grown-up texters" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** three identical-sounding words on the whiteboard — "the words grown-ups muddle most, even in texts. Become the expert!" Retrieval: *they are* squashes into…? (*they're*, Lesson 11).
- **Explain (3–9):** meanings + hidden-word hooks on a table — *there* has **here** inside (place), *their* has **heir** inside (an owner — belonging), *they're* = *they are* (apostrophe for the missing *a*). The best test: "can I say *they are*?" Modelled on "They're going to their house over there."
- **Example (9–14):** shared gap-fills with the test said aloud — "___ my best friends" (*They're*) and "I like ___ new car" (*their*).
- **Check (14–17):** "which means 'belonging to them'?" — multipleChoice; probe for the *heir* clue; both wrong paths scripted.
- **Practice (17–22):** adaptive 9-item bank — place and squash gaps (level 1), belonging gaps, run-the-test items, rainbow pointing (level 2), and stretch: label all three in one sentence, write-your-own all-three sentence, and the End-of-Unit sentence as a pick-the-correct-version item (level 3).
- **Recap (22–25):** child teaches back the three meanings + test; process praise; teaser for *to/too/two*.
