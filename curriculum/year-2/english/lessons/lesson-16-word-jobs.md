# Lesson 16 — Nouns, adjectives and verbs (word jobs)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 4 (Word classes & sentence types)** · 25 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can spot whether a word is a noun, an adjective or a verb."
*(Human view of [`lesson-16-word-jobs.json`](./lesson-16-word-jobs.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "word detective" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** every word has a JOB — nouns name, adjectives describe, verbs do. Detective badge on; retrieval read of *The happy dog ran.*
- **Explain (3–9):** the three detective tools — noun: "can I put *the* in front?"; verb: "can I do it / *I am ___ing*?"; adjective: "does it describe a noun?" Modelled on *dog, run, fluffy, ball, jump, red* — plus the secret: **is/are are quiet BEING verbs** (pre-empts "verb = doing word only").
- **Example (9–14):** shared categorize of *school, sleep, happy* — child must say which tool proved each.
- **Check (14–17):** which word is the verb in *The happy dog ran?* → *ran*. Wrong-answer playbook: *dog* (noun/verb confusion), *happy* (adjective/verb confusion).
- **Practice (17–22):** adaptive 9-item bank — two-column sorts and the-test MC (level 1); three-way sorts, find-the-verb, **being-verb** items and word→job matching (level 2); stretch: all three classes in one sentence, a sort with *is/are* hidden in it, and write-your-own-and-prove-it (level 3).
- **Recap (22–25):** child recites the three tests + the being-verb secret; process praise ("you ran a test on every word instead of guessing").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach + drop to two columns · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a being-verb item and a find-the-verb item.

**Optional video (reinforce):** [Noun Song from Grammaropolis - "Noun Town" — Grammaropolis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kcJzh6gqGM) *(verified 2026-07-03)*. Task: whisper "NOUN!" every time the song names a person, place or thing.

**Differentiation:** support — two columns first, concrete words, test prompt on screen · stretch — three classes in one sentence, being-verb hunts.
**Evidence:** sorts 6+ words with the tests, finds the verb in a sentence, names *is* as a (being) verb.
