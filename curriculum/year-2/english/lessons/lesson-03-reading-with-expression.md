# Lesson 3 — Reading on sight & reading with expression
**English · Year 2 · Unit 1 (Fluent reading: longer words & expression)** · 25 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can read a sentence smoothly, with expression, not robot-by-word."
*(Human view of [`lesson-03-reading-with-expression.json`](./lesson-03-reading-with-expression.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "robot vs storyteller" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** the same sentence read two ways — flat robot vs smooth storyteller. "Which one was the real reader?" Warm-up speak block: beat-read *playground*.
- **Explain (3–8):** **sight words** (the, was, said, you — eyes know them instantly) on flashcards, plus the two-read secret: "Read it once to crack it, then read it again like a storyteller." Punctuation bosses the voice: `.` tiny breath · `?` voice UP · `!` excitement.
- **Example (8–13):** the four-sentence passage (*The dog ran fast. It saw a cat! Where did the cat go? It hid in the box.*) read together twice — decode pass then fluency pass; child's storyteller read judged via a speak block.
- **Check (13–16):** "What does it mean to read a word *on sight*?" → instantly, no sounding out. Wrong-answer playbook: vague "you look at it" and robotic demonstrations.
- **Practice (16–22):** adaptive 9-item speak-heavy bank — short-sentence re-reads and punctuation basics (level 1), question-lift / exclamation-pop speak items, punctuation match, sight-word spotting (level 2), full-passage storyteller reads, in-character giant performance and a brand-new passage (level 3). Covers robot-reading and mark-ignoring misconceptions.
- **Recap (22–25):** child teaches back the two-step secret; process praise for re-reading and voice control.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → model-and-echo re-teach + shorter sentence · bias to **speak** items — expression is judged by listening.

**Video:** none — modelling the two voices is the tutor's own job here. (Unit 1's verified video is on Lesson 1.)

**Differentiation:** support — 3–4 word decodable sentences, model-echo-solo · stretch — a paragraph performed in character.
**Evidence:** child reads a 3-sentence passage smoothly on the fluency pass, honouring the full stop and question mark, and explains "on sight" as instant reading.
