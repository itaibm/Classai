## Flow (all AI, "word-builder" frame — meaning is the star)

- **Hook (0–3):** one root, two OPPOSITE words: help → **helpful** (full of help 👍) vs **helpless** (without help 🤷). Retrieval: what's a suffix?
- **Explain (3–9):** slideshow builds all five from care/kind/quick/enjoy: careful (**⚠️ -ful has ONE l!**), careless, kindness (-ness = a 'thing'), quickly (-ly = how), enjoyment (-ment = the result). Plus the y special: happy → happiness.
- **Example (9–14):** shared building — hopeful/hopeless (the silent e STAYS: -ful starts with a consonant), painful/painless, sadness. Big question seeded: careful — how many l's?
- **Check (14–16):** care + -ful → **careful, one l**. Wrong-answer playbook: *carefull* (copied full's two l's), *carful* (broke the root).
- **Practice (16–22):** adaptive 10-item bank — suffix-meaning match, helpful, fearless (level 1), kindness/quickly/enjoyment (enjoy keeps its y — vowel before it!) + build-and-say-a-sentence speak item (level 2), stretch: **happiness/happily** (y → i meets the new tails) and explain the *carefull* trap (level 3).
- **Recap (22–25):** child states -ful/-less meanings + the one-l trap, says their favourite built word aloud; Unit 2 complete.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → reteach + match-not-spell level 1 · mastery = 2+ correct builds incl. a one-l -ful word + one word used in a sentence.

**Misconceptions covered:** *carefull/helpfull* (two l's) · *carful* (changed the root) · *happyness* (forgot y → i) · *enjoiment* (changed a y that has a vowel before it).

**Differentiation:** support — match roots to suffix cards, -ful/-less pair first · stretch — happiness + happily with meanings.
**Evidence:** builds 2+ real meaning-suffix words (one -ful with a single l) and uses one in a sentence.
**Unit close:** End-of-Unit 2 check next session — spell hopping, hoped, happier; build careful, kindness, happily (4 of 5 = mastery).
