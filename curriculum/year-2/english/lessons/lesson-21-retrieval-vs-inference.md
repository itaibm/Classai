# Lesson 21 — Retrieval vs inference (be a reading detective)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 5 (Comprehension & composition)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can tell a retrieval question from an inference question."
*(Human view of [`lesson-21-retrieval-vs-inference.json`](./lesson-21-retrieval-vs-inference.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, detective frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** detective framing + the Mia passage on screen ("Mia ran into the kitchen… her tummy rumbled loudly"). Easy retrieval: "What did Mia open?" → the fridge.
- **Explain (3–8):** the two question kinds, anchored in ONE passage — retrieval = **find it** ("the fridge" is written there), inference = **work it out** (tummy rumbled → hungry). Key-term cards + comparison table.
- **Example (8–12):** shared categorize — sort 4 questions about Mia into find-it vs work-it-out, incl. the subtle "Why did she open the fridge?" (event written, reason hidden).
- **Check (12–16):** fresh passage (Ben at the bus stop, blue coat) + the classic diagnostic: "'What colour was the coat?' — retrieval or inference?" Must pass the **finger test** ("I can point to 'his blue coat'").
- **Practice (16–19):** solo case on the Patch-the-dog passage, drawing from the 9-item bank — always "show me the words" or "show me the clue".
- **Recap (19–20):** child says the rule aloud (speak block); process praise for pointing at evidence.

**Misconceptions targeted:** everything labelled "inference"; retrieval answered with a memory-guess instead of the text; inference claimed with no clue ("I just know").

**Practice bank:** 9 items — L1 label with the finger test (3), L2 sort 4 questions / locate the exact answer words / spot the worry clue (3), L3 write your own retrieval + inference questions, and the event-vs-reason trap (3). Every item: 2 wrong answers diagnosed, 2 hints, scripted reteach.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → reteach with highlighted words + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. located evidence and a why-question.

**Video:** none — the passages and sorting blocks carry the lesson.

**Differentiation:** support — retrieval answers pre-highlighted, two questions at a time · stretch — write both question types yourself.
**Evidence:** labels retrieval vs inference on a fresh passage, locating words/clues.
