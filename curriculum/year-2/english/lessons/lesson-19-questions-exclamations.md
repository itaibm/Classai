# Lesson 19 — Questions & exclamations (the special marks)
**English · Year 2 · Unit 4 (Word classes & sentence types)** · 20 min · **Delivery: 🤖 fully AI**
**Objective:** "By the end, I can write a question with a ? and a true exclamation starting What/How."
*(Human view of [`lesson-19-questions-exclamations.json`](./lesson-19-questions-exclamations.json) — the JSON is the source of truth. No preparation needed from you.)*

## Flow (all AI, "voice goes UP" frame)

- **Hook (0–3):** *The sky is blue.* flat vs *Is the sky blue?* rising — speak block: child performs the contrast. Retrieval: what ends a telling sentence?
- **Explain (3–8):** two recipes side by side — questions start with a question word (*who/what/where/when/why/how*) or flip the order, ending **?**; true exclamations start **What/How** + big feeling, ending **!**.
- **Example (8–12):** child fixes the broken *Is it raining.* (fillBlank → ?), then co-builds *What a fast dog!* Traps handled: loud statement with !, right form + wrong mark.
- **Check (12–16):** the script's double challenge — *The cake is big.* → *What a big cake!* AND a question about the cake (shortText with sample). Probe: "how does your voice sound?"
- **Practice (16–19):** adaptive 9-item bank — choose/fix the mark (level 1); write a question from the word bank, spot the true exclamation, rebuild a jumbled question (ordering), perform the rising voice (speak) (level 2); stretch: one statement → both special types, **why Stop! is a command not an exclamation**, and a 3-question mini interview (level 3).
- **Recap (19–20):** child states both rules; process praise ("you let your voice check the mark").

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses on a skill → scripted re-teach with the word bank + level 1 · mastery = 3+ correct incl. a self-written ? question and a true What/How exclamation.
