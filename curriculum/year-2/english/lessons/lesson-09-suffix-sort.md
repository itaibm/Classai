## Flow (all AI, "Spelling Sorting Office" frame — retrieval of Lessons 5–8)

- **Hook (0–3):** the Sorting Office opens — four rule-bags (Just add · Drop the e · Double · y → i). Retrieval: name all four rules.
- **Explain (3–8):** the three-question routine, modelled aloud: **silent e? → drop · short vowel + one consonant? → double · ends in y? → y → i · else just add.** Think-aloud sorts: bake, sit, cry, play.
- **Example (8–13):** guided sort of the sneaky pairs — **hope vs hop** and **happy vs play** (play's y has a vowel before it → just add) via a live categorize mat.
- **Check (13–16):** run + -ing → name the rule AND spell it: **double → running**. Wrong-answer playbook: *runing* (mis-sort), cross-rule mix-ups.
- **Practice (16–22):** interleaved 11-item bank — two-bag sorts, name-the-rule, match words→rules (level 1), the **full 8-word sort** + spell-its (hoping/hopping/cried) + read-aloud pair check (level 2), stretch: *playing* (vowel-before-y trap), reject *jumpping* (rule where none applies), and the "see → seeing" trick word (level 3).
- **Recap (22–25):** child recites the three sorting questions aloud; process praise for expert sorting.

**Adaptivity:** start level 2 · 2 right → up · 2 misses → reteach + narrow to a two-bag sort · mastery = 6+ of 8 sorted correctly AND spelled correctly (esp. hoping/hopping).

**Misconceptions covered:** hope/hop cross-rule mix-ups · *plaiing* (y-rule where a vowel precedes the y) · *jumpping* (adding a rule where none applies) · sorting right but not applying the rule.

**Differentiation:** support — three columns, pre-grouped cards · stretch — the "fits no bag" trick word (see → seeing).
**Evidence:** sorts 6 of 8 words correctly and spells the suffixed forms (hoping, hopping, cried, playing).
