## The year in one paragraph

Year 2 is the year children **stop learning to read and start reading to learn**, move into the
**hundreds** in maths, and begin to **work like real scientists, historians, geographers, artists,
musicians and programmers**. Phonics is completed and reading becomes fluent and thoughtful,
driven by a spine of whole books from around the world; handwriting becomes joined; writing is
organised in paragraphs. In maths the **column method** and the **bar model** arrive, with the 3,
4 and 8 times tables, fractions and tenths, and measurement to the minute and the millimetre.
Children run fair tests, dig into the ancient world from Göbekli Tepe to Shang China, map rivers
and volcanoes, learn their first melodic instrument (recorder), keep a sketchbook, code in Scratch,
speak their second year of Spanish, and practise the habits — emotional, physical, financial and
civic — of a healthy, kind, capable person.

## Sample weekly timetable

22 lessons a week, 20–30 minutes each, built from the Planning Brief's time budget. English and
maths come first each morning when attention is freshest; active and practical subjects are spread
through the week so no day is all desk work.

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival (10 min) | Spanish greeting, date and weather routine; reading corner | same | same | same | same |
| 1 | English | English | English | English | English |
| 2 | Maths | Maths | Maths | Maths | Maths |
| Break | Outdoor play | Outdoor play | Outdoor play | Outdoor play | Outdoor play |
| Fluency (10 min) | Times tables + handwriting | Spelling + number facts | Times tables + handwriting | Spelling + number facts | Weekly "personal best" sprints |
| 3 | Science | History | Science | Geography | Music |
| 4 | Spanish | Art & Design | Computing | Spanish | Life Skills |
| 5 | PE & Health | — (independent reading and class read-aloud) | PE & Health | — (independent reading and class read-aloud) | Class read-aloud and celebration |
| End of day (15 min) | Class novel read-aloud | Class novel read-aloud | Class novel read-aloud | Class novel read-aloud | Class novel read-aloud |

Counts: English 5, Maths 5, Science 2, Spanish 2, PE & Health 2, History 1, Geography 1, Art 1,
Music 1, Computing 1, Life Skills 1 = **22**. For home-schooling families using the Classai tutor,
the same weekly counts can be spread over four or five shorter days; keep English and maths daily.

**Daily non-negotiables outside the lessons:** 10–15 minutes of independent reading, a 15-minute
read-aloud of the class text, 5 minutes of maths fluency (counting in 3s/4s/8s/50s/100s, tables),
5 minutes of handwriting or spelling, and at least 60 minutes of physical activity across the day
(PE, play, active travel).

## Vertical alignment

- **From Year 1 (ages 6–7):** place value to 100 and 2/5/10 tables; Phase 5 phonics and early
  Phase 6 suffixes; sentence punctuation; simple fair observations; significant people and local
  history; continents and oceans; first Spanish (greetings, numbers, colours); percussion and
  singing; ScratchJr; naming feelings.
- **To Year 3 (ages 8–9):** place value to 10,000 and all tables to 12 × 12; decimals from tenths;
  area from perimeter; longer novels and writing for effect; classification, digestion, states of
  matter, sound and electricity; Ancient Greece and Rome; the water cycle and biomes; Spanish food,
  hobbies, time and -ar verbs; recorder/ukulele; Scratch selection and variables.
