## The year in one paragraph

Year 3 is the year children become **fluent, independent readers of whole novels**, **secure in every
times table to 12 × 12**, and **investigators** who ask questions and test them. The number system opens
out to 10,000 and past the decimal point; writing is organised in paragraphs for real readers; science
moves to fair tests every unit; and the humanities travel across three continents — Ancient Greece, the
Roman Empire, the Kingdom of Kush and Ancient China, the Amazon, the Mediterranean and West Africa.
Children begin to *explain* as well as *do*: every lesson ends with a check that asks "why?" or
"show me", not just "what?".

## How Year 3 connects

- **From Year 2 (ages 7–8):** place value to 1,000 and the 2/3/4/5/8/10 tables; tenths as fractions;
  phonics complete, simple paragraphs and speech marks; Stone Age to Iron Age, Egypt, Sumer and the
  Indus; latitude, hemispheres and rivers; recorder B-A-G; Scratch sequences and events; Spanish
  greetings, numbers to 31 and colours. See [`../year-2/`](../year-2/).
- **To Year 4 (ages 9–10):** numbers to a million, long multiplication, thousandths and percentages;
  persuasive writing, relative clauses and cohesion; life cycles, materials, Earth and space, forces;
  Anglo-Saxons, Vikings and the Maya; mountains, volcanoes and settlements; perspective, printmaking
  and sculpture; singing in parts; selection and variables in code; food, hobbies and directions in
  Spanish. See [`../year-4/`](../year-4/).

## Cross-curricular threads this year

- **Ancient worlds:** Greek myths in English ↔ Ancient Greece in history; Roman engineering in history ↔
  the Mediterranean in geography; *Where the Mountain Meets the Moon* ↔ Ancient China; Kush ↔ the Nile.
- **Water:** the water cycle (science) → water supply and use (geography) → hydration (PE & Health) →
  the Amazon making its own rain.
- **Ghana:** cocoa and Fairtrade (geography) ↔ Kente and El Anatsui (art) ↔ call-and-response and
  drumming (music) ↔ kpanlogo (dance).
- **Sound:** vibration and pitch (science) ↔ panpipes and instruments (music) ↔ recording audio (computing).
- **Data:** bar charts and line graphs (maths) ↔ weather data (science) ↔ fieldwork (geography) ↔
  branching databases (computing).
- **Kindness and belonging:** *The Boy at the Back of the Class* (English) ↔ upstanders and identity
  (life skills) ↔ online kindness (computing).

## Assessment across the year

Low-stakes and frequent: every lesson has a check for understanding, every unit an end-of-unit mastery
task, and every subject a list of end-of-year mastery checks. Maths and English hold a retrieval and
assessment point at the end of each term; art, music, PE and life skills are assessed by watching,
listening and talking, never by quizzes. A portfolio (September baseline → July) shows each child's growth.
