# Maths Progressions — UK NC ↔ US CCSS ↔ IB PYP

Concept-by-concept progression tables for primary maths (ages 5–11). Each row shows: the **idea**, the **UK NC Year**, the matching **US CCSS code(s)**, and the **IB PYP phase/strand**.

**Code format:** all CCSS maths codes carry the prefix `CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.` (omitted in tables for brevity). So `3.NF.A.1` = `CCSS.MATH.CONTENT.3.NF.A.1`.

**Remember the offset:** US Grade ≈ UK Year − 1, but CCSS often places content one grade *higher* than the naive offset (it is less accelerated than UK NC in several places). **Trust the per-row code.** See `README.md`.

**IB PYP maths strands** (5): *Number; Pattern and function; Measurement; Shape and space; Data handling*. IB has **4 phases** (1–4). Phase mapping is *approximate* — IB does not fix phases to ages: Phase 1 ≈ ages 3–5, Phase 2 ≈ 5–7, Phase 3 ≈ 7–9, Phase 4 ≈ 9–11.

Codes below verified against thecorestandards.org. Leaf codes for grades 1–2 and K–4 geometry are domain-confirmed canonical codes (marked *dc* = domain-confirmed where not page-read).

## 7. Ratio, Proportion & Early Algebra

This strand barely exists as statutory content in UK primary until **Year 6**, and in CCSS is mostly a **Grade 6** topic — i.e. just past the 5–11 band. Included for the top end of primary.

| Idea | UK NC Year | US CCSS code(s) | Grade | IB PYP (strand · phase) |
|------|-----------|------------------|-------|--------------------------|
| Patterns, sequences (informal) | Y1–Y5 | (woven through OA: 4.OA.C.5 generate patterns) | Gr 4 | Pattern & function · Ph 2–4 |
| Ratio & proportion; relative sizes; scale factors | Y6 (10–11) | 6.RP.A.1, 6.RP.A.2, 6.RP.A.3 | Gr 6 | Pattern & function · Ph 4 |
| Simple algebra — formulae, missing-number equations, sequences | Y6 (10–11) | 6.EE.A.1–4, 6.EE.B.5–8, 6.EE.C.9 | Gr 6 | Pattern & function · Ph 4 |

*Note: UK NC Y6 includes a genuine ratio/proportion and simple-algebra unit. The honest CCSS match (6.RP, 6.EE) is **Grade 6** — one of the clearest cases where UK primary reaches into what CCSS calls middle school. Tag accordingly and don't claim a Grade 5 code.*

---

### Cross-cutting note for the tutor
The progressions agree far more than they differ on **order** (count → place value → operations → fractions → proportion). They differ on **timing**: UK NC front-loads times-tables, formal written methods, and a Y6 ratio/algebra unit; CCSS spreads these later. IB PYP refuses to fix any of it to an age. When a parent gives a target standard, find the *concept* row first, then read across — never reverse-engineer the concept from a grade label alone.
