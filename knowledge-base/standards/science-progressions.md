# Science Progressions — UK NC ↔ US NGSS ↔ IB PYP

Concept-by-concept progression tables for primary science (ages 5–11): biology, chemistry, physics, earth & space, plus working scientifically.

**There is no CCSS for science.** The US standards here are **NGSS** (Next Generation Science Standards), coded `<grade>-<discipline><topic>-<n>` — e.g. `3-LS1-1` = Grade 3, Life Science, topic 1, performance expectation 1. Disciplines: **PS** = Physical Science, **LS** = Life Science, **ESS** = Earth & Space Science, **ETS** = Engineering, Technology & Applications. The leading digit/letter **is the grade**, so placement is unambiguous.

**Remember the offset:** US Grade ≈ UK Year − 1 (Year 3 ≈ Grade 2). See `README.md`.

**IB PYP science** is **not a standalone subject** — it is integrated into the programme of inquiry, most strongly under the theme **"How the world works"** (physical/material/energy phenomena) and **"Sharing the planet"** (living things, ecosystems, environment). PYP has no science content codes; the mapping below names the **theme** and (where relevant) the strand of inquiry.

All NGSS codes verified against nextgenscience.org.

## 1. Biology — Living Things, Plants, Animals & Humans

| Idea | UK NC Year | US NGSS code(s) | Grade | IB PYP theme |
|------|-----------|------------------|-------|---------------|
| What plants/animals need; basic needs; name common animals & body parts | Y1 (5–6) | K-LS1-1 (what animals/plants need); K-ESS3-1 | K | Sharing the planet |
| Plant & animal structures & survival/behaviour; offspring resemble parents | Y1–Y2 (5–7) | 1-LS1-1, 1-LS1-2 (structures), 1-LS3-1 (inheritance/traits) | Gr 1 | How the world works / Sharing the planet |
| Habitats, food chains, micro-habitats; biodiversity | Y2 (6–7) | 2-LS2-1, 2-LS2-2 (pollination/seed dispersal), 2-LS4-1 (biodiversity) | Gr 2 | Sharing the planet |
| Nutrition, skeletons & muscles; plants — water transport, parts of a flower | Y3 (7–8) | 3-LS1-1 (life cycles); plant/human anatomy mostly UK-specific | Gr 3 | How the world works |
| Living things & habitats (classification keys); digestion, teeth; food chains | Y4 (8–9) | 4-LS1-1, 4-LS1-2 (structures for survival/info) | Gr 4 | Sharing the planet |
| Life cycles (plants & animals); reproduction; human change to old age | Y5 (9–10) | 5-LS1-1 (plants get materials from air/water), 5-LS2-1 (food webs) | Gr 5 | How the world works / Sharing the planet |
| Classification (Linnaean); evolution & inheritance; adaptation | Y6 (10–11) | 3-LS3-1/2 (traits & environment), 3-LS4-1/2/3/4 (adaptation/fossils) | Gr 3 (NGSS places evolution earlier) | Sharing the planet |

*Note: NGSS places **adaptation, inheritance and fossils in Grade 3** (3-LS3, 3-LS4), whereas UK NC holds formal **evolution & inheritance to Year 6**. This is a clear sequencing divergence — match by concept, and flag that NGSS introduces it ~3 years earlier in label terms.*

## 5. Working Scientifically ↔ NGSS Science & Engineering Practices (SEPs)

UK NC names **5 types of scientific enquiry**; NGSS names **8 Science & Engineering Practices** (Appendix F). They are not 1:1, but map as follows. IB PYP frames the same skills as the **"transdisciplinary skills"** (research, thinking, self-management) and the inquiry cycle.

| UK NC enquiry type | Best-matching NGSS SEP(s) | IB PYP |
|--------------------|----------------------------|--------|
| Observing over time | SEP 3 (Planning & carrying out investigations), SEP 4 (Analyzing & interpreting data) | Research / Thinking skills · inquiry cycle |
| Identifying, classifying & grouping | SEP 4 (Analyzing data), SEP 8 (Obtaining/communicating info) | Research skills |
| Pattern seeking | SEP 4 (Analyzing & interpreting data), SEP 5 (Using maths & computational thinking) | Thinking skills |
| Comparative & fair testing | SEP 3 (Planning & carrying out investigations), SEP 1 (Asking questions/defining problems) | Research / Self-management |
| Researching using secondary sources | SEP 8 (Obtaining, evaluating & communicating information) | Research skills |
| (UK: predict, measure, record, conclude, evaluate) | SEP 2 (Models), SEP 6 (Constructing explanations), SEP 7 (Argument from evidence) | Thinking / Communication skills |

**The 8 NGSS SEPs (verified, Appendix F):** 1. Asking questions & defining problems · 2. Developing & using models · 3. Planning & carrying out investigations · 4. Analyzing & interpreting data · 5. Using mathematics & computational thinking · 6. Constructing explanations & designing solutions · 7. Engaging in argument from evidence · 8. Obtaining, evaluating & communicating information.

**Engineering design** (NGSS-specific, no UK NC primary twin — closest is D&T): **K-2-ETS1-1/2/3** and **3-5-ETS1-1/2/3**. IB PYP folds design/problem-solving into "How we organize ourselves" and "How the world works".

### Cross-cutting note for the tutor
Science shows the **biggest sequencing divergence** of any subject. NGSS front-loads several big ideas relative to UK NC: **light & sound (1-PS4, Grade 1)** vs UK Y3/Y4/Y6; **adaptation/inheritance/fossils (3-LS3/3-LS4, Grade 3)** vs UK Y6 evolution. So the naive "Grade ≈ Year − 1" offset is *least* reliable in science — **always match by concept first, then attach the code.** And note that **NGSS PEs bundle content + a practice + a crosscutting concept**, so one PE often spans what UK NC splits into "content" and "working scientifically".
