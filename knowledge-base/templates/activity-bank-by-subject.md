# Subject-Specific Activity & Game Bank

Signature activities for each subject — the moves that don't generalise neatly but are too good to lose. Use alongside `activity-bank-generic.md` (topic-agnostic frames). Same fields throughout:

> **Name** · **Goal / when** · **How to run** · **Adapt to any topic** · **Classai block(s)** · **Primary example**.

All are doable **1:1 at home**. *Display blocks are look-only; the interactive block carries the answer.*

Subjects: [Maths](#maths) · [English](#english-reading--writing) · [Science](#science) · [History](#history) · [Geography](#geography) · [World Language / Spanish](#world-language--spanish) · [Art](#art) · [Music](#music) · [Computing](#computing) · [Life Skills](#life-skills)

## Maths

### M1. Number Talks
- **Goal / when:** Hook / fluency. Builds mental strategies and flexible thinking.
- **How to run:** (1) Show one calculation (e.g. 18 + 17). (2) Child solves **mentally** and explains *how* — not just the answer. (3) Tutor records 2–3 different strategies and compares them ("which was efficient?").
- **Adapt to any topic:** any mental calculation — addition, ×, fractions of amounts, doubling. The point is the *strategy talk*.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry` for the answer + `speak`/`shortText` for the method; `whiteboard` to record strategies.
- **Primary example:** "18 + 17 — how did you do it?" (Made 20 + 15; or 18 + 18 − 1; or 17 + 17 + 1.)

### M2. Splat!
- **Goal / when:** Practice / retrieval. Inverse thinking — find the hidden part.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a total and a picture with some dots "splatted" (covered). (2) Child works out how many are hidden. (3) Vary which part is splatted.
- **Adapt to any topic:** part-whole anything — number bonds, missing addends, missing factors, fractions of a whole.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry`; `image`/`emojiViz` to show the splat.
- **Primary example:** "There are 10 dots. The splat covers some, 6 show. How many under the splat?" (4.)

### M3. Which One Doesn't Belong (numbers/shapes)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / reasoning. (Maths flavour of the generic WODB.)
- **How to run:** (1) 2×2 grid of numbers or shapes. (2) Child justifies which doesn't belong — *every* one should have a reason. (3) Collect properties (even, prime, square, factors).
- **Adapt to any topic:** numbers, shapes, graphs, fractions.
- **Classai block(s):** `image`/`emojiViz` to show; `speak`/`shortText` for reasoning.
- **Primary example:** 9, 16, 25, 43 — surfaces square numbers, odd/even, single/double digit.

### M4. Bar-Model Stories
- **Goal / when:** Explain / example. Turns word problems into a visual structure.
- **How to run:** (1) Read a word problem. (2) Draw it as bars (part-part-whole or comparison). (3) Child reads the answer off the model, then writes the calculation.
- **Adapt to any topic:** addition/subtraction, multiplication, ratio, fractions, "more than/fewer than" problems.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard` (`animate` to build the bars); `numberEntry` for the answer.
- **Primary example:** "Sam has 12 marbles, 5 more than Jo. How many has Jo?" → bar of 12 with a 5-chunk = 7.

### M5. Estimate-Then-Check (Esti-Mysteries)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / number sense. Estimate, then clues narrow it.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a jar/quantity; child gives a reasonable estimate range. (2) Reveal clues ("it's even," "more than 30"). (3) Each clue updates the estimate until the answer's found.
- **Adapt to any topic:** counting, measurement, money, time.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry`; `steps` to reveal clues one at a time; `image`.
- **Primary example:** "How many cubes? Clue 1: between 20 and 40. Clue 2: a multiple of 5. Clue 3: even." (→ 30.)

### M6. Make / Beat the Target
- **Goal / when:** Practice / fluency. Open-ended combination practice.
- **How to run:** (1) Give a target and digit cards/dice. (2) Child combines with operations to reach it, finding many ways. (3) Beat your previous count of solutions.
- **Adapt to any topic:** four operations, order of operations, money totals.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry`; `shortText` to list solutions.
- **Primary example:** "Use 3, 4, 5, 6 once each to make 24." (e.g. 6×4×(5−3+... ); find several.)

### M7. Shape Hunt / Property Detective
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Geometry in the real world.
- **How to run:** (1) Hunt the house for shapes/angles/symmetry. (2) Child names each and states a property ("a tin is a cylinder — 2 circular faces"). (3) Sort finds by property.
- **Adapt to any topic:** 2D/3D shapes, angles, symmetry, lines.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`/`shortText` to report; `categorize` to sort by property.
- **Primary example:** "Find three things with a right angle." Child reports a book corner, a window, a tile.

### M8. Roll & Calculate
- **Goal / when:** Practice / fluency. Dice-driven, endless fresh problems.
- **How to run:** (1) Roll dice. (2) Apply today's operation (×, place value, rounding). (3) Score rounds; race the clock.
- **Adapt to any topic:** all four operations, place value, rounding, fractions.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry`; `whiteboard` to show the roll.
- **Primary example:** Roll two dice → make the largest 2-digit number, then round to nearest 10.

## English (reading & writing)

### E1. Sentence Stealing / Sentence Imitation
- **Goal / when:** Practice / explain. Learn craft by copying a great sentence's *structure*.
- **How to run:** (1) Take a strong model sentence from a book. (2) Child writes a new sentence with the **same structure** but new content. (3) Compare; name the technique used.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any grammatical structure — fronted adverbials, complex sentences, similes, lists.
- **Classai block(s):** `shortText`; `richText` to display the model.
- **Primary example:** Model: "Silently, the cat crept along the wall." Child: "Suddenly, the dog dashed across the road." (Fronted adverbial.)

### E2. Frayer Vocabulary (race)
- **Goal / when:** Explain / consolidation. Deep word knowledge in four quadrants.
- **How to run:** (1) Centre a word; four boxes: definition, characteristics, examples, non-examples. (2) Child fills all four. (3) "Non-examples" is the powerful box — forces boundary thinking.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any new vocabulary in any subject.
- **Classai block(s):** `table`/`whiteboard` for the grid; `shortText`/`fillBlank` per quadrant.
- **Primary example:** Word "mammal": def (warm-blooded, fur, milk), examples (dog, whale), non-examples (frog, robin).

### E3. Reading Detective (inference)
- **Goal / when:** Check / practice. Reading between the lines with evidence.
- **How to run:** (1) Read a short passage. (2) Ask an inference question ("How is the character feeling? How do you know?"). (3) Child answers **and** quotes the evidence — "point + evidence."
- **Adapt to any topic:** any text — fiction, non-fiction, poems.
- **Classai block(s):** `shortText`/`speak`; `richText` to display the passage.
- **Primary example:** "The boy's hands were shaking as he opened the door." → "He's nervous, because his hands were shaking."

### E4. Word Building / Morpheme Mix
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Prefixes, suffixes, root words.
- **How to run:** (1) Give root words and affix cards. (2) Child combines to make real words and defines them. (3) Spot which combos aren't real words and why.
- **Adapt to any topic:** spelling, vocabulary, etymology.
- **Classai block(s):** `fillBlank`/`matchPairs`; `shortText` to define.
- **Primary example:** "un- + happy = unhappy (not happy); re- + play = replay." Reject "un-jump."

### E5. Show-Don't-Tell Swap
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Descriptive writing.
- **How to run:** (1) Give a "telling" sentence ("He was scared"). (2) Child rewrites to **show** it through action/senses. (3) Build a bank of these.
- **Adapt to any topic:** emotions, settings, characters.
- **Classai block(s):** `shortText`; `speak`.
- **Primary example:** "She was angry." → "She slammed the book down and stormed out."

### E6. Punctuation Doctor (error-spot)
- **Goal / when:** Check. Editing skill.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a sentence with errors. (2) Child finds and fixes each. (3) Explain *which rule* each fix applies.
- **Adapt to any topic:** capitals, full stops, commas, apostrophes, speech marks.
- **Classai block(s):** `shortText` (rewrite) or `multiSelect` (find all errors).
- **Primary example:** "my freind dosnt like dogs" → "My friend doesn't like dogs." (capital, spelling, apostrophe.)

### E7. Story Mountain / Plot Map
- **Goal / when:** Explain / consolidation. Story structure for reading and planning.
- **How to run:** (1) Map a story onto: opening → build-up → climax → resolution → ending. (2) Child places events; or plans their own. (3) Use to plan writing.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any narrative — reading comprehension or writing planning.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering` (sequence events); `whiteboard` for the mountain.
- **Primary example:** Map "The Three Little Pigs" onto the five stages.

### E8. Read-Aloud Fluency Race
- **Goal / when:** Practice / fluency. Decoding + expression.
- **How to run:** (1) Child reads a short passage aloud. (2) Tutor notes accuracy + expression; child re-reads to beat their own smoothness. (3) Repeated reading builds fluency.
- **Adapt to any topic:** phonics, sight words, poetry, any text.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak` (AI listens/judges); `richText` to display the text.
- **Primary example:** Re-read a poem three times, adding expression each pass.

## Science

### S1. Predict–Observe–Explain (POE)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / explain / practice. The core scientific reasoning loop.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a setup; child **predicts** what'll happen and why. (2) Do it (or watch a demo video); child **observes**. (3) Child **explains** any gap between prediction and result.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any demonstrable phenomenon — floating/sinking, dissolving, magnets, shadows.
- **Classai block(s):** `multipleChoice`/`shortText` for the prediction; `video`/`image` for the observation; `speak`/`shortText` for the explanation.
- **Primary example:** "Will this orange float or sink?" Predict → test (it floats) → explain (peel traps air).

### S2. Classify-It (keys & groups)
- **Goal / when:** Check / practice. Classification is a core science skill.
- **How to run:** (1) Give specimens/cards. (2) Child sorts using a branching key (yes/no questions) or into given groups. (3) Build their own key for a new set.
- **Adapt to any topic:** animals, plants, materials, rocks, forces.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`; `ordering` for key steps; `trueFalse` per branch.
- **Primary example:** Sort animals by "Has a backbone?" then "Lays eggs?" — vertebrate groups.

### S3. Fair-Test Design
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Planning an investigation.
- **How to run:** (1) Pose a question ("Does a bigger sail make a faster boat?"). (2) Child identifies what to **change** (variable), **measure** (outcome), and **keep the same** (controls). (3) Spot why an unfair version is unfair.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any investigable question.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize` (change / measure / keep same); `shortText`; `multipleChoice` (which test is fair?).
- **Primary example:** "Which paper towel soaks up most?" Change = brand; measure = water absorbed; keep same = size, water amount.

### S4. Spot the Variable (fair/unfair)
- **Goal / when:** Check. Quick diagnostic of fair-testing understanding.
- **How to run:** (1) Describe an experiment. (2) Child says if it's fair, and if not, **what was changed that shouldn't have been**.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any experimental scenario.
- **Classai block(s):** `trueFalse` + `shortText`.
- **Primary example:** "I tested ramps but used a heavier ball on the steep one." → unfair (changed two things).

### S5. Label & Function (diagram)
- **Goal / when:** Check / consolidation. Anatomy/systems.
- **How to run:** (1) Show an unlabeled diagram. (2) Child labels parts and states each part's **job**. (3) "What if this part broke?" deepens it.
- **Adapt to any topic:** plant parts, human body, circuits, the Sun-Earth-Moon system.
- **Classai block(s):** `image` (display); `fillBlank`/`matchPairs` to label; `shortText` for function.
- **Primary example:** Label a flower (petal, stamen, stigma) and say what each does in pollination.

### S6. Sorting: Living / Non-living / Reversible / Materials
- **Goal / when:** Check. Property-based classification.
- **How to run:** (1) Items + property boxes. (2) Child sorts and justifies boundary cases (is a seed living?). (3) Re-sort by a different property.
- **Adapt to any topic:** living things, materials, changes (reversible/irreversible), states of matter.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`.
- **Primary example:** Sort changes: melting (reversible), burning (irreversible), freezing (reversible).

### S7. Model-It (analogy build)
- **Goal / when:** Explain. Make the invisible visible with a physical model.
- **How to run:** (1) Build a model of an abstract process. (2) Child manipulates it to show the concept. (3) Discuss where the analogy breaks.
- **Adapt to any topic:** orbits (torch + ball), circuits (a loop of string), food chains (linked arrows).
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard`/`image` to show; `speak` to explain.
- **Primary example:** Use a lamp (Sun), a ball (Earth) to model day/night by spinning the ball.

## History

### H1. Source Detective
- **Goal / when:** Check / practice. Using evidence — the historian's core skill.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a source (photo, object, document). (2) Child says what it tells us, what it doesn't, and how reliable it is. (3) "Who made this and why?" surfaces bias.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any period with artefacts or images.
- **Classai block(s):** `image` (display); `shortText`/`speak`; `multipleChoice` for "what can we infer?"
- **Primary example:** A photo of a Victorian factory → infer working conditions, note what it leaves out.

### H2. Then vs Now Sort
- **Goal / when:** Hook / check. Change over time, very concrete for young children.
- **How to run:** (1) Cards of objects/jobs/daily life. (2) Child sorts into "then" / "now" / "both." (3) Discuss what changed and why.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any historical period vs today; toys, transport, school, homes.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`.
- **Primary example:** Sort: candle, lightbulb, horse, car, washboard, washing machine.

### H3. Chronology / Timeline Race
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Sense of period and order.
- **How to run:** (1) Event cards out of order. (2) Child places on a timeline; race to do it correctly. (3) Add "what came between?" and cause links.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any set of dated events or periods.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering`; `numberLine` for the timeline.
- **Primary example:** Order: Stone Age → Bronze Age → Iron Age → Romans → Anglo-Saxons.

### H4. Hot-Seat (in role)
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. Recall + empathy.
- **How to run:** (1) Child becomes a historical figure/ordinary person. (2) Tutor interviews; child answers in role using facts learned. (3) Swap.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any period or figure.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`; `shortText`.
- **Primary example:** Child is an Egyptian scribe: "What's your job? What do you write on?" (papyrus, hieroglyphs).

### H5. Cause & Consequence Web
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Historical reasoning beyond dates.
- **How to run:** (1) Centre an event. (2) Child maps causes (before) and consequences (after) around it. (3) Rank which cause mattered most (Diamond-9 link).
- **Adapt to any topic:** any major event.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard` for the web; `ordering`/`categorize` for cause vs consequence.
- **Primary example:** The Great Fire of London — causes (wood, drought, wind) and consequences (rebuilt in brick).

### H6. Artefact Mystery
- **Goal / when:** Hook. Curiosity + inference.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a mystery object from a period. (2) Child guesses its use from clues. (3) Reveal; discuss what it tells us about life then.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any period with distinctive objects.
- **Classai block(s):** `image`; `multipleChoice`/`shortText` for the guess.
- **Primary example:** Show a Roman strigil → "What's this for?" (scraping oil/dirt off skin — no soap).

### H7. Day-in-the-Life Diary
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. Writing + recall in context.
- **How to run:** (1) Child writes a short diary as a person from the period. (2) Must include 3 accurate details learned. (3) Read aloud.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any period or culture.
- **Classai block(s):** `shortText`; `speak`.
- **Primary example:** "A day as a WWII evacuee" — gas mask, train, new family, rationing.

## Geography

### G1. Map Detective
- **Goal / when:** Check / practice. Map-reading skills.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a map. (2) Ask directed questions — find a feature, give directions, read symbols/keys, estimate distance. (3) Child uses compass points and the key.
- **Adapt to any topic:** local maps, world maps, atlases, plans.
- **Classai block(s):** `image` (display map); `multipleChoice`/`shortText`/`fillBlank` for answers.
- **Primary example:** "Which direction is the church from the school? What symbol shows the woods?"

### G2. Continents & Oceans Sort/Bingo
- **Goal / when:** Practice / retrieval. Locational knowledge.
- **How to run:** (1) Bingo grid or sort cards of countries/features. (2) Tutor calls clues; child marks/sorts. (3) Build toward placing things on a blank map.
- **Adapt to any topic:** continents, oceans, countries, capitals, biomes.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`; `matchPairs` (country↔continent); `multiSelect` for the grid.
- **Primary example:** Sort countries into continents: Brazil→S. America, Kenya→Africa, Japan→Asia.

### G3. Compare Two Places
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. The core KS2 geography skill (similarity/difference).
- **How to run:** (1) Two places (home town vs a contrasting locality). (2) Child compares climate, jobs, landscape, food using a two-box/Venn. (3) Explain a reason for one difference.
- **Adapt to any topic:** UK vs another country, city vs countryside, two biomes.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize` (A / both / B); `table` to display.
- **Primary example:** Compare your town with a village in Kenya — water, school, weather, homes.

### G4. Physical vs Human Sort
- **Goal / when:** Check. A key geographical distinction.
- **How to run:** (1) Feature cards (mountain, bridge, river, factory). (2) Child sorts into physical (natural) vs human (made). (3) Boundary cases (a reservoir?) spark discussion.
- **Adapt to any topic:** landscapes, settlements, features.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`.
- **Primary example:** Sort: river, road, hill, harbour, forest, town → physical vs human.

### G5. Weather & Climate Tracker
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Real data over time.
- **How to run:** (1) Child records local weather daily (or reads a chart). (2) Spot patterns; compare to another climate zone. (3) Link weather to seasons/latitude.
- **Adapt to any topic:** seasons, climate zones, water cycle.
- **Classai block(s):** `table`/`numberEntry` to log; `emojiViz` for a simple chart.
- **Primary example:** Track a week's temperatures; compare to the Sahara and the Arctic.

### G6. Where in the World? (20 Questions, geo)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / retrieval. Locational reasoning.
- **How to run:** (1) Tutor picks a place. (2) Child asks yes/no questions (hemisphere, continent, coastal?) to find it. (3) Reverse roles.
- **Adapt to any topic:** countries, capitals, landmarks.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`/`trueFalse` for the Q&A.
- **Primary example:** "Is it in Europe? Is it an island?" narrowing to Iceland.

### G7. Build-a-River / Landform Model
- **Goal / when:** Explain. Physical geography made concrete.
- **How to run:** (1) Model a river (source → mouth) with objects or a drawing. (2) Child labels stages and features. (3) "What happens when it floods?"
- **Adapt to any topic:** rivers, mountains, coasts, the water cycle.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard` (`animate`); `fillBlank` to label.
- **Primary example:** Draw a river: source, tributary, meander, mouth — label each.

## World Language / Spanish

### L1. TPR (Total Physical Response)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / practice. Link words to movement — powerful for early vocab.
- **How to run:** (1) Tutor says a command in Spanish + does the action ("salta" + jumps). (2) Child copies, then responds to the word alone. (3) Build up a sequence; speed it up.
- **Adapt to any topic:** verbs, body parts, classroom commands, directions.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak` (child responds/repeats); `image`/`video` to model the action.
- **Primary example:** "Salta / corre / aplaude" — child performs each on hearing it.

### L2. Vocab Speaking Race (beat the clock)
- **Goal / when:** Retrieval / fluency. Rapid recall under gentle pressure.
- **How to run:** (1) Pick a category (colours, animals). (2) Child names as many in Spanish as they can in 60s. (3) Record; beat it next time.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any vocabulary set.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak` (AI counts correct words); the app logs the score.
- **Primary example:** "How many colours in Spanish in a minute?" rojo, azul, verde…

### L3. Describe-and-Draw
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Listening + vocabulary in context.
- **How to run:** (1) Tutor describes a simple scene in Spanish. (2) Child draws what they hear. (3) Compare drawing to intended scene; swap roles.
- **Adapt to any topic:** colours, numbers, prepositions, objects.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard` (child draws); `speak` for the description.
- **Primary example:** "Un gato negro en la mesa" → child draws a black cat on a table.

### L4. Picture-Word Match / Memory
- **Goal / when:** Practice / retrieval. Link new word to image (not English).
- **How to run:** (1) Cards: Spanish word ↔ picture. (2) Match or play face-down memory. (3) Avoid English — go word↔image directly.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any concrete vocabulary.
- **Classai block(s):** `matchPairs`; `flashcards`.
- **Primary example:** Match "manzana" to an apple picture, "perro" to a dog.

### L5. Simon Says / Simón Dice
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Listening + commands, playful.
- **How to run:** (1) Tutor gives commands in Spanish; child only acts on "Simón dice…". (2) Builds command vocab + listening. (3) Swap.
- **Adapt to any topic:** body parts, actions, directions.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`; `image` to model.
- **Primary example:** "Simón dice: toca la cabeza" (touch your head).

### L6. Mini-Dialogue Role-Play
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. Real communicative use.
- **How to run:** (1) Set a scene (café, greeting). (2) Pair acts a short dialogue using set phrases. (3) Swap roles; vary one detail.
- **Adapt to any topic:** greetings, ordering food, introductions, shopping.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`; `fillBlank` for a scaffolded script.
- **Primary example:** "¿Cómo te llamas?" / "Me llamo… ¿Y tú?" — practise the exchange.

### L7. Number/Colour Bingo (in target language)
- **Goal / when:** Practice / retrieval. Listening recognition.
- **How to run:** (1) Grid of numbers/colours. (2) Tutor calls in Spanish; child marks. (3) Race to fill a line.
- **Adapt to any topic:** numbers, colours, days, months.
- **Classai block(s):** `multiSelect`/`categorize`; `table` to display.
- **Primary example:** Tutor calls "siete," child marks 7.

## Art

### A1. Element Hunt (line/shape/colour)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / explain. Notice the building blocks of art.
- **How to run:** (1) Choose an element (line, shape, colour, texture, pattern). (2) Child finds it in a painting or around the house. (3) Name and describe examples.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any element/principle of art.
- **Classai block(s):** `image` (artwork); `speak`/`shortText`; `categorize` to sort by element.
- **Primary example:** Find three kinds of line in a Van Gogh (swirling, jagged, thick).

### A2. Colour-Mixing Lab
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Primary→secondary, tints/shades.
- **How to run:** (1) Predict what two colours make. (2) Mix (real paint or describe). (3) Record results; predict the next.
- **Adapt to any topic:** colour theory, warm/cool, tints/shades.
- **Classai block(s):** `multipleChoice` (predict the result); `image` to show; `numberLine` for tint→shade.
- **Primary example:** "Blue + yellow = ?" → predict, mix → green.

### A3. Artist in the Style Of
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. Apply a technique by imitation.
- **How to run:** (1) Study an artist's signature technique. (2) Child makes their own piece in that style. (3) Name the techniques they used.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any artist or movement (pointillism, cubism, Kandinsky shapes).
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard` (digital sketch); `image` for the model; `speak` to explain choices.
- **Primary example:** A dot painting "like Seurat" — build an image from dots of colour.

### A4. Describe-the-Artwork (visual literacy)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / check. Look closely and talk about art.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a painting. (2) Child describes what they see, then what they think/feel, then wonders. (3) "What's happening? How did the artist make you feel that?"
- **Adapt to any topic:** any artwork.
- **Classai block(s):** `image`; `speak`/`shortText`.
- **Primary example:** Look at *The Hay Wain* — notice, interpret, wonder.

### A5. Draw-What-You-Hear/Feel
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Creative mark-making.
- **How to run:** (1) Play music or name an emotion. (2) Child draws lines/colours that match. (3) Discuss the choices.
- **Adapt to any topic:** mood, music, abstract expression.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard`; `speak` to explain.
- **Primary example:** "Draw how this happy/sad music feels" — fast bright lines vs slow dark ones.

### A6. Sketch-from-Observation
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Looking and drawing skill.
- **How to run:** (1) Pick a real object. (2) Child draws what they **see**, not what they "know" (slow looking). (3) Add one detail of tone or texture.
- **Adapt to any topic:** still life, nature, portraits.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard`/`image`; `speak` to reflect.
- **Primary example:** Draw an apple from life — its real shape, shadow, and stalk.

## Music

### M-1. Rhythm Echo / Clap-Back
- **Goal / when:** Hook / practice. Pulse and rhythm.
- **How to run:** (1) Tutor claps a short rhythm. (2) Child echoes it back exactly. (3) Lengthen; child then invents one for the tutor.
- **Adapt to any topic:** beat, rhythm, note values.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`/`custom` (child claps/taps, AI or tutor listens); `image` for notation.
- **Primary example:** Clap "ta-ta-ti-ti-ta"; child echoes, then creates one.

### M-2. High/Low, Fast/Slow Sort
- **Goal / when:** Check. Musical opposites (pitch, tempo, dynamics).
- **How to run:** (1) Play sounds. (2) Child sorts/labels: high vs low, fast vs slow, loud vs soft. (3) Move along a continuum.
- **Adapt to any topic:** pitch, tempo, dynamics, timbre.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`; `numberLine` (soft→loud); `audio` via `video`.
- **Primary example:** Sort animal sounds by pitch: mouse (high) vs lion (low).

### M-3. Name That Instrument / Sound
- **Goal / when:** Retrieval. Timbre recognition.
- **How to run:** (1) Play an instrument sound. (2) Child names it and its family. (3) Match sound→picture→family.
- **Adapt to any topic:** instruments, families (strings, brass, percussion).
- **Classai block(s):** `multipleChoice`; `matchPairs` (sound↔instrument); `video` for the clips.
- **Primary example:** Hear a violin → name it → "strings."

### M-4. Compose a Pattern
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Create and perform.
- **How to run:** (1) Give a grid (4 beats × symbols). (2) Child arranges sounds into a pattern. (3) Perform it; vary one element.
- **Adapt to any topic:** rhythm, melody (high/low), structure (ABA).
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering`/`categorize` for the grid; `custom`; `whiteboard` to notate.
- **Primary example:** Build a 4-beat rhythm with claps and stamps, then perform it.

### M-5. Loud/Soft Conductor
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Dynamics, control, listening.
- **How to run:** (1) Child "conducts" — big gesture = loud, small = soft. (2) Tutor (or a song) follows. (3) Swap; add fast/slow.
- **Adapt to any topic:** dynamics, tempo, expression.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`/`custom`; `numberLine` (soft→loud scale).
- **Primary example:** Conduct "Twinkle Twinkle" getting louder then softer.

### M-6. Steady Beat Games
- **Goal / when:** Hook / practice. Internalise pulse.
- **How to run:** (1) Play a song with a clear beat. (2) Child keeps the steady beat (march, tap, pass an object on each beat). (3) Distinguish beat from rhythm.
- **Adapt to any topic:** pulse, tempo.
- **Classai block(s):** `speak`/`custom`; `video` for the song.
- **Primary example:** Pass a beanbag on every beat of a marching song — drop = missed beat.

## Computing

### C1. Unplugged Algorithm (robot game)
- **Goal / when:** Hook / explain. Algorithms with no computer.
- **How to run:** (1) Child gives the tutor ("the robot") precise step-by-step instructions to do a task. (2) Tutor follows **literally** — vague steps fail, which is the lesson. (3) Debug the instructions.
- **Adapt to any topic:** sequencing, algorithms, debugging.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering` (arrange the steps); `speak`/`shortText`.
- **Primary example:** "Make a jam sandwich" — child instructs; tutor takes everything literally (spreading jam on the unopened jar).

### C2. Spot the Bug (debugging)
- **Goal / when:** Check / practice. Finding and fixing errors.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a sequence of instructions with a bug. (2) Child predicts what'll go wrong, finds the bug, fixes it. (3) Re-run mentally.
- **Adapt to any topic:** algorithms, code blocks, logic.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering`/`multipleChoice`; `steps` to show the code.
- **Primary example:** Directions to a treasure with one wrong turn — find and fix it.

### C3. Sequence the Steps
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Algorithmic ordering.
- **How to run:** (1) Give jumbled instruction cards. (2) Child orders them into a working algorithm. (3) Test it on paper.
- **Adapt to any topic:** any process — a recipe, a getting-dressed routine, a game's rules.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering`.
- **Primary example:** Order the steps to draw a square with a turtle: forward, turn, ×4.

### C4. Pattern / Loop Spotter
- **Goal / when:** Explain / check. Repetition (loops) and pattern.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a repeating sequence. (2) Child spots the repeat and rewrites it as "repeat N times." (3) Shorter is better — that's the loop's value.
- **Adapt to any topic:** loops, patterns, efficiency.
- **Classai block(s):** `fillBlank`/`shortText`; `multipleChoice`.
- **Primary example:** "forward, turn, forward, turn, forward, turn, forward, turn" → "repeat 4 times: forward, turn."

### C5. Binary / Code Cracking
- **Goal / when:** Hook / practice. Data representation.
- **How to run:** (1) Teach a simple code (binary dots, A=1 cipher, pixel art grid). (2) Child decodes a message, then encodes one. (3) Making is harder than breaking.
- **Adapt to any topic:** binary, pixels, ciphers, ASCII.
- **Classai block(s):** `fillBlank`/`numberEntry`; `table`/`whiteboard` for a pixel grid.
- **Primary example:** Colour a 5×5 grid from a code to reveal a pixel picture.

### C6. Stay-Safe Scenario Sort
- **Goal / when:** Check / consolidation. Online safety.
- **How to run:** (1) Give scenario cards. (2) Child sorts "safe / not safe / ask an adult" and explains. (3) Discuss what to do.
- **Adapt to any topic:** e-safety, passwords, personal info, kindness online.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`; `multipleChoice`.
- **Primary example:** Sort: "share your password with a friend" (not safe), "tell an adult about a mean message" (safe).

### C7. Flowchart a Decision
- **Goal / when:** Explain / practice. Selection (if/then).
- **How to run:** (1) Take an everyday decision. (2) Child draws a flowchart with a yes/no branch. (3) Trace different paths.
- **Adapt to any topic:** selection, conditions, logic.
- **Classai block(s):** `whiteboard`; `ordering`; `multipleChoice` (which branch?).
- **Primary example:** "Is it raining? → yes: take a coat / no: don't." Draw and trace.

## Life Skills

### LS1. Play Shop (money & change)
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Real-world maths + money handling.
- **How to run:** (1) Price some items. (2) Child is shopkeeper or customer — totals the cost, pays, gives/checks change. (3) Vary amounts; use real coins.
- **Adapt to any topic:** money, addition, subtraction, budgeting.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry`; `speak`; `image` of coins.
- **Primary example:** "Two items at 35p and 40p, you pay £1 — how much change?" (25p, counted out.)

### LS2. Clock / Timetable Challenge
- **Goal / when:** Practice. Telling time, planning a day.
- **How to run:** (1) Give times or a daily schedule. (2) Child reads/sets times, works out durations ("how long until lunch?"). (3) Plan a pretend day.
- **Adapt to any topic:** time, duration, schedules.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberEntry`; `image`/`table` for clocks/timetables; `ordering` to sequence a day.
- **Primary example:** "It's 3:15. Tea is at 5:00. How long to wait?" (1 hr 45 min.)

### LS3. Sort the Routine (sequencing tasks)
- **Goal / when:** Practice / consolidation. Independence and planning.
- **How to run:** (1) Cards of a routine's steps. (2) Child orders them sensibly. (3) Spot what goes wrong if out of order.
- **Adapt to any topic:** getting ready, cooking, crossing a road, tidying.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering`.
- **Primary example:** Order morning routine: wake → wash → dress → breakfast → teeth → bag.

### LS4. Healthy Plate Sort
- **Goal / when:** Check / consolidation. Nutrition.
- **How to run:** (1) Food cards. (2) Child sorts into food groups and builds a balanced plate. (3) Discuss "sometimes" foods.
- **Adapt to any topic:** food groups, healthy eating, balanced diet.
- **Classai block(s):** `categorize`; `image` of a plate.
- **Primary example:** Sort: apple (fruit/veg), bread (carbs), chicken (protein), cake (treat).

### LS5. What-Would-You-Do? (decisions & feelings)
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. Social/emotional reasoning.
- **How to run:** (1) Pose a social scenario. (2) Child says what they'd do and why; consider others' feelings. (3) Explore alternatives — there's rarely one answer.
- **Adapt to any topic:** friendship, conflict, emotions, safety, kindness.
- **Classai block(s):** `multipleChoice` (options) + `speak`/`shortText` (reasoning).
- **Primary example:** "Your friend is left out of a game. What could you do?" — discuss kind options.

### LS6. Feelings Thermometer / Zones
- **Goal / when:** Hook / consolidation. Emotional literacy and regulation.
- **How to run:** (1) Show a scale (calm → frustrated → angry). (2) Child places a feeling/situation on it and names a calming strategy for each level. (3) Practise the strategy.
- **Adapt to any topic:** emotions, self-regulation, empathy.
- **Classai block(s):** `numberLine`/`ordering`; `categorize`; `speak`.
- **Primary example:** "Where on the thermometer is 'someone took my toy'? What helps you come back down?" (breathe, count, ask for help).

### LS7. Plan a Real Task (budget / recipe / trip)
- **Goal / when:** Consolidation. Applied planning across skills.
- **How to run:** (1) Give a small real project with a constraint. (2) Child plans steps, costs, and order. (3) Carry it out if possible; review.
- **Adapt to any topic:** budgeting, cooking, organising, problem-solving.
- **Classai block(s):** `ordering` (steps); `numberEntry` (budget); `shortText` (plan).
- **Primary example:** "Plan a £5 picnic for two — what do you buy, and does it fit the budget?"
