# Recommended Video Library (by subject)

Curated, **real** video sources for every subject in this knowledge base, for children ages 5–11. Researched and verified to channel/platform level (June 2026).

### How to use this — and the honesty rule
Channels and platforms are stable; individual video URLs are not (they get deleted, renamed, re-uploaded). So this library recommends **official channels, playlists and curriculum platforms by name + their official link**, and tells you *what to search for within them*. When the Classai tutor wants a `video` block, it should use one of these named sources and the given search term. **It must not invent a specific `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID** — if it can't resolve a real video, it should build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` itself instead (see [`INGESTION.md`](INGESTION.md) §5 and [`methods/technology-and-multimedia.md`](methods/technology-and-multimedia.md) on active video).

**Always preview before showing a child**, and pair every clip with a watching task + a retrieval check afterwards — passive watching teaches little.

The two best **UK-curriculum-aligned** video sources, useful for *every* subject below, are:
- **BBC Bitesize** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize — KS1/KS2 video clips indexed by exact NC topic.
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy — full free filmed lessons, by subject and year.
- **BBC Teach** (YouTube) — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach — curriculum-mapped class clips.

## 1. Maths

**Best channels**
- **Numberblocks** (official, BBC Studios) — https://www.youtube.com/@Numberblocks — number sense, counting, place value, number bonds, +/−, early ×. *Perfect for KS1 / Years 1–2.* Maps to maths `year-1.md`, `year-2.md`.
- **Numberblocks & Numberblocks "Learn to Count"** playlists — search the channel for the exact number/operation.
- **Math Antics** — https://www.youtube.com/@mathantics — clear whiteboard lessons on fractions, decimals, place value, area/perimeter, long multiplication/division. *Years 3–6.* Maps to upper-KS2 maths + `extension-topics.md`.
- **Math with Mr. J** — https://www.youtube.com/@MathwithMrJ — short, single-skill "how to" videos (e.g. "adding fractions with unlike denominators"). Great for a targeted reteach of one step.
- **Khan Academy** — https://www.youtube.com/@khanacademy — structured arithmetic→pre-algebra; **Khan Academy Kids** app for Reception/Y1.
- **Scratch Garden** — https://www.youtube.com/@ScratchGarden — catchy maths songs (skip counting, times tables, shapes) for KS1.

**Search-term examples (within BBC Bitesize / the channels):** "place value tens and ones", "equivalent fractions", "column subtraction with regrouping", "telling the time", "Roman numerals", "mean average".

## 2. English (reading, phonics, grammar)

**Best channels**
- **Alphablocks** (official, BBC/CBeebies) — https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC_qs3c0ehDvZkbiEbOj6Drg — systematic synthetic phonics (Phases 2–5), blending and segmenting. *The strongest free phonics source; KS1 / Years 1–2.* Maps to English `year-1.md`, `year-2.md`.
- **Geraldine the Giraffe** (Mr Thorne) — search "Geraldine the Giraffe phoneme" — one clear video per grapheme/phoneme; excellent for a single sound.
- **Grammarsaurus** — https://www.youtube.com/@Grammarsaurus — SPaG (spelling, punctuation, grammar) mapped to the UK NC: fronted adverbials, apostrophes, word classes. *Years 3–6.*
- **Grammaropolis** — https://www.youtube.com/@Grammaropolis — parts of speech as characters/songs (nouns, verbs, adjectives…).
- **BBC Teach – English / Primary** — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach — comprehension, writing, poetry clips.
- **Oak National Academy – English** — filmed lessons on reading comprehension and writing genres.

**Search-term examples:** "split digraph magic e", "fronted adverbials", "inference reading", "using a comma in a list", "writing a setting description".
