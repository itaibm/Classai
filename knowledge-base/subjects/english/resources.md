# English — Curated Free Resources (KS1–KS2)

Real, stable, free resources for primary English, mapped to platform + exact topic title. Use these to seed `video`/`image` blocks. **Never invent specific video IDs** — reference the platform and the exact topic/playlist name, and let the tutor resolve a real URL (or fall back to a self-built slideshow/whiteboard if it can't).

> Rule of honesty (see INGESTION.md §5): name a **stable platform + exact search/topic title**, prefer **channel/playlist** over a single video ID, and never guess a URL.

---

## BBC Bitesize — KS1 English (ages 5–7)
Free, curriculum-aligned, with short articles, videos and quizzes. Search the exact topic title on bbc.co.uk/bitesize.

- **Phonics (KS1)** — *Blending sounds to read words*, *Segmenting words*, *Digraphs and trigraphs*, *Split digraphs*, *Tricky / common exception words*.
- **Spelling (KS1)** — *Plurals (-s and -es)*, *Adding -ing, -ed, -er and -est*, *Suffixes -ful, -less, -ly, -ment, -ness*.
- **Grammar & punctuation (KS1)** — *Capital letters and full stops*, *Joining words (conjunctions)*, *Nouns, adjectives and verbs*, *Expanded noun phrases*, *Statements, questions, commands and exclamations*, *Apostrophes*, *Conjunctions (co-ordination and subordination)*.
- **Reading (KS1)** — *Making predictions*, *Finding clues / inference*.
- **Writing (KS1)** — *How to write a story*, *How to write instructions*.

## BBC Bitesize — KS2 English (ages 7–11)
- **Spelling (KS2)** — *Prefixes*, *Suffixes -ly, -ous and -tion*, *Common spellings (Year 3/4 word list)*, *Words ending in -tion, -sion, -ssion, -cian*, *Words ending in -able and -ible*, *Silent letters*, *i before e*, *Tricky spellings (-cious/-tious, -cial/-tial)*.
- **Grammar (KS2)** — *Adverbs*, *Prepositions*, *Using a and an*, *Standard English*, *Verb tenses*, *Fronted adverbials*, *Apostrophes for possession (plurals)*, *Relative clauses*, *Modal verbs*, *Adverbs of possibility*, *Active and passive voice*, *The subjunctive*, *Formal and informal writing*.
- **Punctuation (KS2)** — *Inverted commas / speech marks*, *Parenthesis (brackets, dashes and commas)*, *Semicolons, colons and dashes*, *Hyphens*, *Bullet points / colons in lists*.
- **Reading (KS2)** — *Inference / reading between the lines*, *Summarising a text*, *Similes, metaphors and personification*, *Fact and opinion*, *Comparing texts*, *Analysing language*.
- **Writing (KS2)** — *Organising writing into paragraphs*, *Writing a recount*, *Describing settings and characters*, *Writing an explanation text*, *Persuasive writing*, *Cohesion and linking ideas*, *Writing dialogue / using speech*, *Planning, drafting and editing writing*.
- **Homophones (KS2)** — *Homophones (there, their, they're)*.

## Oak National Academy — oaknational.academy
Free, full sequenced English lesson units with video and worksheets for every year group. Search the year + unit title.
- **Year 1 English** — *Common exception words*, *Writing a sentence*, *Plural noun suffixes*.
- **Year 2 English** — *Sentence types*, *Writing instructions*, fluency and spelling suffix units.
- **Year 3 English** — *Spelling (prefixes & suffixes)*, *Writing structure / paragraphs*, *Determiners*, reading comprehension units.
- **Year 4 English** — *Standard English*, *Explanation texts*, reading comprehension units.
- **Year 5 English** — *Modal verbs*, *Writing to persuade*, reading comprehension units.
- **Year 6 English** — *Formal and informal language*, *Editing and proof-reading*, *Writing: summarising*, reading comprehension units.

## Phonics Play — phonicsplay.co.uk
Free section has well-known interactive phonics games for blending and segmenting (Phases 2–6).
- ***Buried Treasure*** and ***Picnic on Pluto*** — read/sort real and "alien" words (blending & segmenting practice).
- **Phase 3 / Phase 5 grapheme games** — practise specific digraphs/trigraphs and alternative spellings.

## Phonics & early reading on the BBC
- **BBC Bitesize — *Phonics* (KS1 collection)** — short phonics video clips and activities.
- **Alphablocks (BBC iPlayer / CBeebies / official YouTube channel)** — animated phonics: blending CVC words, digraphs, and the "magic e" episodes. Brilliant for Phase 2–5 introductions.
- **CBeebies Bedtime Stories** — read-aloud picture-book stories: perfect for pausing to predict, infer and discuss.

## Spelling practice
- **Spelling Frame — spellingframe.co.uk** — free spelling rules, word lists and games organised by NC rule number for Years 1–6 (covers the Y3/4 and Y5/6 statutory word lists, /shun/, -able/-ible, silent letters, ei/ie). Reference the exact rule/word-list title.

## Writing stimulus
- **Pobble 365 — pobble365.com** — a new, high-quality image every day with story starters, questions and sentence challenges. Excellent for narrative, description, recount and figurative-writing prompts at any year.

## Grammar & punctuation teaching
- **Grammarsaurus — grammarsaurus.co.uk** — free grammar and punctuation explainer videos and resources mapped to year groups: word classes, conjunctions, apostrophes, fronted adverbials, possessive apostrophes, relative clauses, parenthesis, semicolons/colons, passive voice, hyphens, cohesive devices. (Some content is paid; the free YouTube channel and sample packs cover the basics.) Reference the exact topic title.

## Oracy / spoken language
- **BBC Bitesize KS2 — *Speaking and listening / presenting*** — guidance and clips on speaking clearly, structuring an explanation and presenting.
- **Voice 21 / oracy resources** — free guidance for families on building talk, discussion and presentation skills.

---

### How the tutor should use these
1. Match the topic being taught to the **exact title** above (e.g. teaching fronted adverbials → "BBC Bitesize KS2 — *Fronted adverbials*").
2. Prefer the platform's **topic/search page or channel** over guessing a specific video URL.
3. If a real, resolvable URL can't be found, **build a slideshow/whiteboard** for the concept instead of inventing a link (per INGESTION.md §5).
