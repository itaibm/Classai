# English — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary English: phonics, reading, grammar (ages 5–11). Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips, set a watching task, follow with a check.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize — KS1/KS2 English clips by NC topic.
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy — filmed reading & writing lessons by year.
- **BBC Teach** — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach

## Best channels
- **Alphablocks** (official, BBC/CBeebies) — https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC_qs3c0ehDvZkbiEbOj6Drg — systematic synthetic phonics (Phases 2–5), blending & segmenting. *The strongest free phonics source; KS1 / Years 1–2.* Maps to `year-1.md`, `year-2.md`.
- **Geraldine the Giraffe (Mr Thorne)** — search "Geraldine the Giraffe phoneme" — one clear video per grapheme/phoneme.
- **Grammarsaurus** — https://www.youtube.com/@Grammarsaurus — SPaG mapped to the UK NC: fronted adverbials, apostrophes, word classes. *Years 3–6.*
- **Grammaropolis** — https://www.youtube.com/@Grammaropolis — parts of speech as characters/songs.
- **Oak National Academy – English** — comprehension and writing-genre lessons.
