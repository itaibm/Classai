# Spanish (KS2) — Curated Free Resources

Real, stable, free platforms for primary Spanish, mapped to the topics in `spanish-ks2.md`. **Platform + exact topic only — no invented video IDs.** When the tutor wants a `video` block it should use these named references; if it cannot resolve a real URL it should fall back to a `speak`/`whiteboard`/`slideshow` it builds itself rather than guess a URL.

> Note on the curriculum fit: BBC Bitesize and Oak National Academy are written to the England National Curriculum. Duolingo, Rockalingua and Light Speed Spanish are general beginner-Spanish resources used here at the *topic* level — pick the matching topic, not a specific paid path.

## Duolingo
Free gamified app (and web) for spaced practice and vocabulary recycling — **Duolingo → Spanish course (from English)**. Not curriculum-mapped, but the early "Spanish for beginners" sections cover greetings, numbers, colours, family, food, animals — ideal as **daily 5-minute recycling** between Classai lessons. Use it for retention, not as the primary teaching path. (Has an under-13 / Duolingo for Schools option for child accounts.)

## BBC Teach
Free classroom video clips — **BBC Teach → Primary → Languages → Spanish**. Short authentic clips (children in Spain, real conversations, songs) useful as listening/intercultural input for greetings, family, food and weather topics. Good for the National Curriculum "intercultural understanding" strand.

## Pronunciation & audio anchors
- **BBC Bitesize** clips include native audio for every topic — use these to model sounds before the child speaks.
- **Forvo** (forvo.com) — free native-speaker pronunciations of individual Spanish words; useful when you need to confirm how one word sounds. Search the exact word.

## How to use these in Classai
- Lead with **listening** (BBC Bitesize audio, Rockalingua song, BBC Teach clip), then move straight to a `speak` block — children must *say* it, not just hear it.
- Use **Duolingo** as out-of-lesson spaced recycling so vocabulary from earlier topics keeps coming back.
- For grammar topics (13, 14), **Light Speed Spanish** gives the clearest child-friendly explanation; follow it with a Classai speaking task.
- Never embed a guessed video URL. If no real link resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` and run a `speak` activity instead.
