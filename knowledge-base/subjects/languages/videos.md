# Spanish — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary Spanish (KS2, ages 7–11). Speaking-first: pause and have the child repeat aloud. Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`speak` activity instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips, set a listening task, follow with a `speak` check.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize KS2 Spanish** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize
- **Oak National Academy – Spanish** — https://www.thenational.academy — filmed lessons.

## Best channels
- **Rockalingua** — https://www.youtube.com/@rockalingua — animated music videos: numbers, colours, greetings, weather, family. *Ideal for KS2 vocabulary.* Maps to `spanish-ks2.md`.
- **Calico Spanish** — https://www.youtube.com/@CalicoSpanish — slow, clear puppet skits and beginner songs with good pronunciation.
- **Basho & Friends** — https://www.youtube.com/@bashoandfriends — Spanish-only songs (introductions, food, animals, the alphabet) using repetition. *Upper KS2.*
- **Spanish Playground / The Spanish Experiment** — simple stories and read-alouds.

## Search-term examples
"Spanish numbers 1 to 20 song", "colores en español for kids", "Spanish greetings song", "los animales en español", "días de la semana song".
