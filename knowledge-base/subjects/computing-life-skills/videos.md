# Computing & Life Skills — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary computing/coding and life skills (ages 5–11). Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/platforms by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips, set a watching task, follow with a check.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize KS1/KS2 Computing / PSHE** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy

## Computing & coding — best channels & platforms
- **Scratch Team** (official, MIT) — https://www.youtube.com/@ScratchTeam — official tutorials; pair with the free editor at https://scratch.mit.edu. Maps to `computing-ks1-ks2.md` and `extension-topics.md`.
- **griffpatch** — https://www.youtube.com/@griffpatch — step-by-step Scratch game builds (platformers, quizzes) for keen upper-KS2 coders.
- **Code.org** — https://www.youtube.com/@codeorg + courses at https://code.org — block coding, "How the Internet Works", computational thinking.
- **Barefoot Computing / Hour of Code** — unplugged computational-thinking activities and guided hours.

**Computing search terms:** "Scratch make a sprite move", "what is an algorithm for kids", "Scratch loops repeat block", "how the internet works for kids".

## Life skills — best channels & trusted bodies
- **ThinkUKnow / CEOP Education** — https://www.thinkuknow.co.uk — age-banded online-safety films (e.g. *Jessie & Friends*, *Play Like Share*). Maps to `online-safety.md`.
- **Internet Matters** — https://www.internetmatters.org — digital footprint, screen-time balance.
- **BBC Bitesize – Money / Wellbeing** — needs vs wants, saving, feelings, healthy habits.
- **NHS / Change4Life** — healthy eating, exercise, sleep.
- **Experience AI** (Raspberry Pi Foundation & Google DeepMind) — https://www.experience-ai.org — kid-level "what is AI" (maps to the AI-awareness extension topic).
- **GoNoodle** — https://www.youtube.com/@GoNoodle — movement + emotional-regulation/mindfulness breaks.

**Life-skills search terms:** "needs and wants for kids", "saving money for children", "staying safe online primary", "calming breathing for kids", "growth mindset for children".
