# Science — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary science (ages 5–11). Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips, set a watching task, follow with a check.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize — KS1/KS2 science clips by exact NC unit.
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy — filmed science lessons by year.
- **BBC Teach** — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach

## Best channels
- **SciShow Kids** — https://www.youtube.com/@scishowkids — biology, materials, weather, the body; warm and concrete. *KS1–lower KS2.*
- **Crash Course Kids** — https://www.youtube.com/@crashcoursekids — Earth & space, forces, ecosystems, engineering. *Upper KS2.* Maps to `year-5.md`, `year-6.md`.
- **Mystery Doug / Mystery Science** — https://www.youtube.com/@MysteryScience — short answers to "why" questions; great hooks for any unit.
- **Operation Ouch!** (BBC, the human body) — search "Operation Ouch [topic]" — digestion, teeth, circulation. Maps to `year-4.md`, `year-6.md`.
- **Nat Geo Kids** — https://www.youtube.com/@NatGeoKids — animals, habitats, planets.

## Search-term examples
"states of matter for kids", "the water cycle", "how does a circuit work", "life cycle of a plant", "why do we have day and night", "food chains and webs".
