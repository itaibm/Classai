# Science — Curated free resources (KS1–KS2)

Real, free, well-known platforms only. Each entry names the **platform** and an **exact topic/title** so the tutor can resolve a real page or video without guessing. **No invented video IDs** — prefer the platform's topic page, channel, or playlist. If a specific URL can't be resolved, the tutor should build its own slideshow/whiteboard rather than fabricate a link.

## How to use these
- **BBC Bitesize** and **Oak National Academy** are the closest to the UK National Curriculum — best for "teach the unit properly."
- **Generation Genius**, **Mystery Science** and **Crash Course Kids** are engaging video explainers at the **topic** level (some Generation Genius/Mystery Science content sits behind a school login, but topics are free to find and many lessons have free previews).
- **BBC Teach** has short, free classroom clips.
- Match the resource to the year file's topic; the titles below mirror the topic headings in `year-1.md … year-6.md`.

## Whole-platform anchors (always safe to reference)
- **BBC Bitesize — KS1 Science** — `bbc.co.uk/bitesize/subjects` → Science (KS1). Covers plants, animals, materials, seasons.
- **BBC Bitesize — KS2 Science** — `bbc.co.uk/bitesize/subjects` → Science (KS2). Covers all Y3–Y6 units.
- **Oak National Academy — Science** — `thenational.academy` → Science, by year group (free full lesson sequences with video + worksheets, aligned to the NC).
- **BBC Teach — Primary Science** — `bbc.co.uk/teach` → Primary → Science (short classroom clips).
- **Mystery Science** — `mysteryscience.com` (topic-based lessons; many free "mini-lessons").
- **Generation Genius** — `generationgenius.com` (animated, NGSS/UK-mappable topic lessons; free previews).
- **Crash Course Kids** — YouTube **channel** `@crashcoursekids` (search the channel by topic; do not guess a specific video ID).

## Resource honesty rules (for the tutor)
- Reference resources by **platform + exact topic title**, as above — never invent a specific video ID or URL slug.
- Prefer **channel/playlist/topic pages** to single videos when unsure.
- If a real URL can't be resolved at lesson time, **build a slideshow/whiteboard** from the topic file's "Teach it" content instead of guessing a link.
- All experiments in the year files are **safe for home with a parent**: an adult handles anything hot, sharp or involving heat; **batteries (cells) only — never mains electricity**; **never look at the Sun**; never taste unknown substances.
