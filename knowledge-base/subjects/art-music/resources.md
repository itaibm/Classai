# Art & Music (KS1–KS2) — Curated Free Resources

Real, stable, free platforms for primary Art & Design and Music, mapped to the topics in `art-ks1-ks2.md` and `music-ks1-ks2.md`. **Platform + exact topic only — no invented video IDs.** When the tutor wants a `video`/`image` block it should use these named references; if it cannot resolve a real URL it should fall back to a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` it builds itself, or a hands-on `make`/`perform` activity, rather than guess a URL.

> BBC Bitesize and Oak National Academy are written to the England National Curriculum. Tate Kids (art), BBC Ten Pieces and Chrome Music Lab (music) are used at the *topic* level for activities and listening.

## BBC Ten Pieces
Free flagship primary music programme: short films, arrangements and classroom activities built around a set of real orchestral and contemporary pieces. Hub: **BBC Ten Pieces** (bbc.co.uk/teach/ten-pieces). Best for **listening, appraising and instrument families**:

## Chrome Music Lab
Free, no-login browser experiments from Google — wonderful hands-on tools. Hub: **musiclab.chromeexperiments.com.**
- **Song Maker** — place notes on a grid (higher = higher pitch), build and play back a short piece → Topics 8 (notation), 9 (composing), 3 (pitch).
- **Melody Maker / Rhythm** — drag notes high/low; build rhythms → Topics 3, 2.
- **Spectrogram / Sound Waves** — see what different timbres look like → Topic 6.
- General play with speed/volume → Topic 5 (dynamics & tempo).

## How to use these in Classai
- **Art:** lead with an `image`/`video` of a real artwork (Tate Kids, BBC Bitesize), talk about it using the formal-element words, then run a hands-on **make** activity — the child should *do*, not just watch.
- **Music:** lead with a short **listen** (BBC Ten Pieces, BBC Bitesize), then **perform** with voice/body, then **compose** (Chrome Music Lab Song Maker is ideal for compose-and-playback).
- Never embed a guessed video URL. If no real link resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard`, or just run the practical make/perform/compose activity, which needs no link.
