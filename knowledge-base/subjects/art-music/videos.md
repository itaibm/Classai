# Art & Music — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary art & design and music (ages 5–11). Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips, set a watching/listening task, follow with a "have a go" activity.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize KS1/KS2 Art & Design / Music** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy

## Art & Design — best channels
- **Art for Kids Hub** — https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC5XMF3Inoi8R9nSI8ChOsdQ — step-by-step "draw along" tutorials; achievable and confidence-building. Maps to `art-ks1-ks2.md` drawing topics.
- **Art with Mati & Dada** — search "Art with Mati and Dada [artist]" — ~8-min animated artist intros (Van Gogh, Kandinsky, Klimt, Frida Kahlo). Maps to "studying artists" + `extension-topics.md`.
- **Tate Kids** — https://www.tate.org.uk/kids (+ Tate YouTube) — art history and simple techniques.
- **The Kitchen Table Classroom / Createful Kids** — colour mixing, painting, printmaking projects.

**Art search terms:** "how to draw [animal] step by step", "colour wheel for kids", "Van Gogh for kids", "printmaking for children".

## Music — best channels & tools
- **BBC Ten Pieces** (official, BBC) — https://www.bbc.co.uk/teach/ten-pieces — orchestral music for primary, with listening & composing activities. Maps to "listening & appraising".
- **Chrome Music Lab** — https://musiclab.chromeexperiments.com — free interactive tools for rhythm, pitch, melody (hands-on composing). Maps to "composing".
- **Hoffman Academy** — https://www.youtube.com/@HoffmanAcademy — free, friendly piano/keyboard basics and note-reading.
- **Music Teacher Toolbox / Ready GO Music** — rhythm play-alongs, pulse vs rhythm, note values.

**Music search terms:** "pulse and rhythm for kids", "high and low pitch lesson", "dynamics forte piano for children", "name that instrument".

See also: [`resources.md`](resources.md) for galleries and music-making tools.
