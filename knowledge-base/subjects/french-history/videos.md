# French History — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video **channels and playlists** for the story of France (ages 5–12), named by channel + search term. Part of the per-subject video set. Maps to `subjectKey: history`.

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips (especially Horrible Histories and any Revolution/Napoleon material) for tone & length, set a watching task, follow with a check.

Many of the best French children's-history videos are in **French (FR)** — ideal for a learner also studying the French language. The tutor can play a French clip and narrate/translate alongside, or use the English channels listed.

---

## Best channels for every topic

**French (FR) — first choice for this subject:**
- **Lumni (francetv)** — https://www.lumni.fr (and the **Lumni** YouTube channel) — France's official free educational platform; short, curriculum-aligned *histoire* clips for every period below. Maps to all topics in `french-history.md`.
- **Quelle Histoire** — https://www.youtube.com/@QuelleHistoire — short animated children's-history films per figure/period (Vercingétorix, Clovis, Charlemagne, Jeanne d'Arc, Louis XIV, Napoléon, La Révolution). The single best kid-level French-history channel.
- **TV5MONDE** — https://www.youtube.com/@TV5MONDE (and `apprendre.tv5monde.com`) — French culture and history for learners.

**English:**
- **Horrible Histories** (official, BBC) — https://www.youtube.com/@HorribleHistories — has **"Rotten Romans"** (Roman Gaul background), **"Measly Middle Ages,"** and **French Revolution** songs/sketches; funny and memorable. Parent-preview for tone; keep Revolution violence age-appropriate.
- **BBC Teach** — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach — Roman and medieval life clips (general, applies to France too).
- **Simple History** — https://www.youtube.com/@SimpleHistory — short animated overviews (preview; suits upper-primary) for the French Revolution and Napoleon.
- **National Geographic Kids** — https://www.youtube.com/@NatGeoKids — civilisations and figures (English).
