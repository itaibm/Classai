# French History — Curated Free Resources

Real, stable, free resources for teaching the story of France to children (ages 5–12), named by **platform + exact topic/section title** (never invented video IDs). The tutor should resolve these by searching the named platform for the named title, or point the parent there. When a `video` block can't be resolved to a real URL, fall back to a self-built `slideshow`/`whiteboard` rather than guessing a URL (see `INGESTION.md` §5).

Maps to `subjectKey: history`. Many of the best French children's resources are in **French** — flagged **(FR)** — and are perfect for a learner also studying French; English options are given alongside.

---

## Whole-subject hubs

- **Lumni (francetv) — Histoire** (`lumni.fr`) **(FR)** — France's official free educational platform (from francetv / Éducation nationale): short curriculum-linked clips and articles for *la Préhistoire, les Gaulois, le Moyen Âge, la Révolution* and more, organised by school level.
- **Quelle Histoire** (`quellehistoire.com` / app / YouTube) **(FR)** — a hugely popular French children's-history brand: short animated films, books and an app covering Vercingétorix, Clovis, Charlemagne, Jeanne d'Arc, Louis XIV, Napoléon, La Révolution française, and more. Designed for ages ~6–12.
- **Élysée — "The symbols of the French Republic explained to children"** (`elysee.fr`) — the official French presidency's kid-level guide to the flag, Marianne, La Marseillaise, the motto and 14 juillet (English available).
- **TV5MONDE — Enseigner/Apprendre le français + history culture** (`apprendre.tv5monde.com`, `enseigner.tv5monde.com`) **(FR)** — culture and history clips for French learners.
- **Britannica Kids** (`kids.britannica.com`) — English kid-level reference for Gaul, Clovis, Charlemagne, Joan of Arc, Louis XIV, the French Revolution, Bastille Day, Napoleon.
- **BBC Bitesize** (`bbc.co.uk/bitesize`) — English coverage of the **French Revolution** (causes, the Bastille) at KS3 level; useful background for upper-primary.
- **National Geographic Kids** (`kids.nationalgeographic.com`) — English articles, e.g. **"Joan of Arc."**

## Honesty & safety notes
- Resources are named by **platform + exact title/topic**; the tutor resolves a real URL by searching the named platform, or falls back to a self-built visual. **Never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID.**
- **Parent-preview** Horrible Histories and any Revolution/Napoleon material for tone and length; keep the Revolution's violence **age-appropriate** (name that there was conflict and that people, including the king, were killed — without graphic detail).
- Many top resources are **French**; this is a feature for a learner also studying the French language. Pair a French clip with English narration from the tutor when needed.
- See `videos.md` for recommended **channels/playlists**; this file lists topic-level resource pages and official sites.
