# Maths — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary maths (ages 5–11). Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Channels and platforms are stable; individual video URLs are not. Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**, and never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If the tutor can't resolve a real video, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Always preview clips, set a watching task, and follow with a retrieval check.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize — KS1/KS2 maths clips by exact NC topic.
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy — full filmed maths lessons by year.
- **BBC Teach** — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach

## Best channels
- **Numberblocks** (official, BBC Studios) — https://www.youtube.com/@Numberblocks — number sense, counting, place value, number bonds, +/−, early ×. *KS1 / Years 1–2.* Maps to `year-1.md`, `year-2.md`.
- **Math Antics** — https://www.youtube.com/@mathantics — clear whiteboard lessons: fractions, decimals, place value, area/perimeter, long ×/÷. *Years 3–6.* Maps to upper-KS2 + `extension-topics.md`.
- **Math with Mr. J** — https://www.youtube.com/@MathwithMrJ — short single-skill "how to" videos; ideal for a targeted reteach of one step.
- **Khan Academy** — https://www.youtube.com/@khanacademy — arithmetic → pre-algebra; **Khan Academy Kids** app for Reception/Y1.
- **Scratch Garden** — https://www.youtube.com/@ScratchGarden — maths songs (skip counting, times tables, shapes) for KS1.

"place value tens and ones", "equivalent fractions", "column subtraction with regrouping", "telling the time", "Roman numerals", "mean average", "area and perimeter".
