# Primary Maths — Curated Free Resources (Years 1–6)

Real, free, well-known platforms named at the **topic/playlist level**. **No invented video IDs or URLs** — the tutor should reference the platform + exact topic title and, when a real link can't be resolved, build its own whiteboard/slideshow instead of guessing a URL.

## The platforms (what each is best for)

- **BBC Bitesize — KS1 & KS2 Maths** — the backbone. Short topic pages (`bbc.co.uk/bitesize`) with explainer text, a video or animation, and a quick activity. Covers every topic below. Search by topic title. Free, UK-curriculum-aligned, no account needed.
- **Oak National Academy — Maths (Year 1–6)** — full free lesson sequences (`thenational.academy`) with video teaching, worksheets and quizzes, organised by year group and unit. Best when you want a complete structured lesson, not just a clip.
- **Khan Academy — Arithmetic / Early Math / Pre-algebra** (`khanacademy.org`) — free practice with instant feedback and mastery tracking; strongest for KS2 fractions, decimals, percentages, and algebra/ratio. (US-organised but topic-aligned; map "grade" loosely to UK year + 1.)
- **NRICH (University of Cambridge)** — `nrich.maths.org` "Primary" — rich, low-floor/high-ceiling problems and investigations for **reasoning and problem-solving**, not drill. Best for stretch and "talk about your thinking."
- **Numberblocks (CBeebies / BBC)** — animated series for **early number** (ages 3–7): each number/idea as a character. Brilliant for counting, cardinality, place value and number bonds in Years R–2. Reference the series/episode theme, not a video ID.
- **Topmarks** — `topmarks.co.uk` — curated free interactive **games** by topic and age (e.g. Hit the Button, Place Value Basketball, Coins, Telling the Time). Best for fluency practice and quick warm-ups.

## How the tutor should use these

1. Prefer a **BBC Bitesize** topic page for a quick explainer + check, or an **Oak National Academy** unit for a full structured lesson.
2. Use **Topmarks** / **Hit the Button** for fluency warm-ups (bonds, tables, time, money).
3. Use **Khan Academy** for KS2 fractions/decimals/percentages/algebra practice with feedback.
4. Use **NRICH** when the child is ready to reason and investigate, not just practise.
5. Use **Numberblocks** for early-number hooks (Years R–2).
6. **Never fabricate a specific video URL or ID.** Name the platform + exact topic title; if a real link can't be resolved, build a whiteboard/slideshow instead.
