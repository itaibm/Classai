# Israel History & Heritage — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for Israel history and heritage (ages 5–12). Part of the per-subject video library (see `../../video-library.md`).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see `../../INGESTION.md` §5). Preview clips for tone and length, set a watching task, follow with a check.

**Even-handedness rule.** For **modern/contested** topics (Zionism, 1948, present-day Israel), prefer **neutral, factual** sources (country profiles, reputable reference) and **parent preview**; do not use advocacy videos from any side.

---

## Best for every topic

- **BimBam** (official channel; archive on **ReformJudaism.org**) — https://bimbam.com — the **single best source** for animated Jewish heritage: Bible stories (Abraham, the Exodus, King David), every festival (Passover, Shavuot, Sukkot, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Hanukkah, Purim, Tu B'Shvat), and Israel. Free, child-friendly. Maps to `ancient-israel.md` and `festivals-and-heritage.md`.
- **BBC Bitesize — "Judaism" (RE)** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize — clips and animations on Jewish beliefs, the Tanakh, the Temple, Jerusalem and the festivals; UK-curriculum, balanced. Maps to all files.
- **National Geographic Kids** — https://www.youtube.com/@NatGeoKids — country and place facts (Israel, the Dead Sea, the Middle East); neutral and engaging. Maps to `diaspora-and-modern.md`.

## Ancient Israel & heritage stories
- **BimBam — Bible / Torah stories** — search "BimBam Abraham," "BimBam Exodus / Passover story," "BimBam King David," "BimBam Maccabees / Hanukkah." Animated heritage narratives. Maps to `ancient-israel.md`.
- **BBC Bitesize — "The Temple" / "Jerusalem in Judaism"** — the First and Second Temples and Jerusalem's place in Jewish life.
- **Israel Museum (Jerusalem)** — https://www.imj.org.il/en — online tours and the **"Model of Jerusalem in the Second Temple period"** for picturing the ancient city (parent-guided).

**Ancient search terms:** "Abraham story for kids," "Exodus Moses for kids," "King David and Goliath for kids," "the First and Second Temple for kids," "Maccabees Hanukkah story."

## Festivals (living history)
- **BimBam — festival films** — search "BimBam Passover," "BimBam Sukkot," "BimBam Shavuot," "BimBam Rosh Hashanah," "BimBam Yom Kippur," "BimBam What Is Hanukkah," "BimBam Purim," "BimBam Tu B'Shvat," "BimBam Yom HaAtzmaut." The **go-to** for festivals. Maps to `festivals-and-heritage.md`.
- **BBC Bitesize — "Jewish festivals"** — Pesach, Shavuot, Sukkot, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Hanukkah explained for children.

**Festival search terms:** "Passover seder for kids," "what is Sukkot for kids," "Hanukkah story for kids," "Purim Queen Esther for kids," "Tu B'Shvat new year of the trees."

## Modern era & symbols (light, balanced — parent preview)
- **BimBam / ReformJudaism.org — "Israel" and "Yom HaAtzmaut" videos** — modern Israel and Independence Day, child-friendly.
- **National Geographic Kids — "Israel country profile" / Middle East** — neutral facts: map, capital, people, flag, the Dead Sea. Maps to `diaspora-and-modern.md`.
- **BBC Bitesize — Israel / Middle East geography & society** — balanced background (parent-guided).

**Modern search terms (use neutral sources, parent preview):** "Israel flag and anthem for kids," "Eliezer Ben-Yehuda Hebrew revival," "Israel country facts for kids."

## Usage notes for the tutor
- Prefer **BimBam** for animated heritage stories and festivals; **BBC Bitesize** for clear, curriculum-matched RE explanations; **National Geographic Kids** for neutral facts about the modern country; the **Israel Museum** for artefacts and "how do we know?"
- For **contested modern topics**, use **neutral, factual** sources only, **parent-previewed**, and pair with the even-handed "*joyful for one people, sorrowful for another*" framing from `diaspora-and-modern.md`.
- Always name the **platform + exact title/search term**; **never fabricate a video ID**. If a real link can't be resolved, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` in-app instead.
- See `resources.md` for books, texts, museum collections and reference articles.
