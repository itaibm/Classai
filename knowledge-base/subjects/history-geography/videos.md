# History & Geography — Recommended Videos

Real, verified video sources for primary history and geography (ages 5–11). Part of the per-subject split of [`../../video-library.md`](../../video-library.md).

**Honesty rule.** Use the **official channels/playlists by name + the search term given**; never invent a `youtube.com/watch?v=…` ID. If no real video resolves, build a `slideshow`/`whiteboard` instead (see [`../../INGESTION.md`](../../INGESTION.md) §5). Preview clips (especially Horrible Histories/Geography Now for tone & length), set a watching task, follow with a check.

**Best for every topic (UK-curriculum-aligned):**
- **BBC Bitesize** — https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize — KS1/KS2 history & geography clips by topic and by skill (sources, chronology, map skills).
- **Oak National Academy** — https://www.thenational.academy — filmed lessons by year.
- **BBC Teach** — https://www.youtube.com/@bbcteach

## History — best channels
- **Horrible Histories** (official, BBC) — https://www.youtube.com/@HorribleHistories — Romans, Egyptians, Vikings, Anglo-Saxons, Tudors; funny and memorable. Maps to `history-ks2.md`.
- **BBC Teach – History** — Great Fire of London, Stone Age, WWII home front.
- **Simple History** — https://www.youtube.com/@SimpleHistory — short animated overviews (preview; suits upper KS2).
- **Khan Academy – Ancient civilisations** — Egypt, Greece, Maya background.

**History search terms:** "Great Fire of London for kids", "Ancient Egypt Nile", "Roman Britain", "Viking longships", "Stone Age Stonehenge".

## Geography — best channels
- **National Geographic Kids** — https://www.youtube.com/@NatGeoKids — continents, biomes, weather, cultures. Maps to `geography-ks1.md`, `geography-ks2.md`.
- **Geography Now!** — https://www.youtube.com/@GeographyNow — one country per episode (upper KS2; preview) for region studies.
- **Ordnance Survey** — https://www.youtube.com/@OrdnanceSurvey — UK map skills, grid references, contours.
- **Free School / MakeMeGenius** — landforms, seasons, Equator & poles for younger children.

**Geography search terms:** "the water cycle", "how volcanoes form", "reading a map grid reference", "rivers source to sea", "climate zones for kids".
