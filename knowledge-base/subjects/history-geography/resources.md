# History & Geography — Curated Free Resources

Real, stable, free resources, named by **platform + exact topic/section title** (never invented video IDs). The tutor should resolve these by searching the named platform for the named title, or point the parent there. When a `video` block can't be resolved to a real URL, fall back to a self-built `slideshow`/`whiteboard` rather than guessing a URL (see `INGESTION.md` §5).

All resources here are aligned to the **UK National Curriculum, KS1–KS2**, and chosen because they're well known, free, and stable.

---

## Whole-subject hubs

- **BBC Bitesize — KS1 History** (`bbc.co.uk/bitesize`): the index of all KS1 history topics with short articles, animations and activities.
- **BBC Bitesize — KS2 History**: the index of all KS2 history topics (Stone Age, Egyptians, Greeks, Romans, Anglo-Saxons, Vikings, Maya, local & beyond-1066 studies).
- **BBC Bitesize — KS1 Geography** and **KS2 Geography**: indexes covering the UK, continents/oceans, maps, weather, rivers, volcanoes, climate, settlements and map skills.
- **Oak National Academy** (`thenational.academy`): free, full sequenced lesson units with slides and video for primary History and Geography, KS1 and KS2 — search the subject then the unit (e.g. "Ancient Egypt", "Rivers").
- **BBC Teach — Primary** (`bbc.co.uk/teach`): short curriculum-linked class clips for History and Geography, grouped by topic.
- **National Geographic Kids** (`natgeokids.com`): kid-level fact pages and images for history civilisations, animals, biomes, countries and physical geography.
- **Ordnance Survey — MapZone / "Map reading made easy, even"** (`ordnancesurvey.co.uk`): free, official map-skills resources, games and guides (symbols, grid references, contours).
- **Horrible Histories** (CBBC / official YouTube channel): humorous, topic-level history clips and songs — use at the **topic level** (e.g. the Romans, Vikings, Egyptians songs); parent-previewed for tone.

## Usage notes for the tutor

- Prefer **BBC Bitesize** for a clear, curriculum-matched written/animated explanation; **Oak National Academy** for a full sequenced lesson; **BBC Teach** and **Horrible Histories** for short video clips; **National Geographic Kids** for engaging facts and images; **Ordnance Survey MapZone** and the **Met Office** for hands-on geography skills.
- For **local history/geography**, the best resources are the family's **own local library, museum, archive and old maps** (e.g. National Library of Scotland georeferenced maps) — always parent-guided.
- Always name the **platform + exact title** when suggesting a resource; never fabricate a specific video URL or ID. If a real link can't be resolved, build the visual in-app instead.
